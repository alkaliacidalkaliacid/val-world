# Character Profile: Priya (Host of the Red Mediatoress)
 
## Baseline Identity
- **Hero Name:** None. The entity she hosts is the **Red Mediatoress**.
- **Real Name:** Priya Raghavan
- **Ancestry:** Indian descent
- **Age:** 43
- **Family:**
  - Husband: **Arjun**, PhD in engineering (Georgia Tech, advanced medical devices); helped Dr. Hermes analyze Val medical gear
  - Daughters: two teenagers (names used in prose: Kavya, the younger; Meera, the elder)
  - Mother-in-law: **Dadi**, openly hostile to Bog
- **Occupation:** Nurse at the Free Clinic in **Florence** (Alabama). In truth closer to a doctor or PhD; her specialty is **superhuman medicine** ("hero medicine"), and some of that knowledge applies to Val physiology.
- **Education:** Trained in hero medicine at **Emory University**.
- **Powers:** The Red Mediatoress (see below). Before that, a lifelong ability to see strange, otherworldly things and sense the presence of spirits.
- **Personality (core):** Timid, nervous, and stuttering in ordinary conversation, with an unexpected intensity when the subject is spirits. Kind and brave when it counts: she fought to pry Dadi's fingers off her daughter, and she stopped the Mediatoress from erasing Bog because he had brought medicine to the children.
### Appearance
Plump, curvaceous woman of Indian descent, usually in a sari and nurse's work clothes.
- **Transformed:** her sari burns away in the red aura, leaving a crimson-and-black costume that clings to her figure (prose adds a gold band at the waist and a wide red sash streaming in a wind that is not there). **Her skin turns red** when she becomes the Red Mediatoress, and her eyes burn red while the entity speaks.
### History
Has seen things since childhood: shapes that were not there, people standing in empty rooms, places that "had something in them." Other children and then adults teased her, and she wondered whether she was losing her mind or had a power. She began researching heroes (how they awakened, what they had in common, whether any of them saw as she does), but the war interrupted the work and she never reached a conclusion. She studied hero medicine at Emory University in Atlanta, where she met Arjun (see below). She has been treating patients at the Florence clinic with Dr. Hermes and has known Bog only as the anonymous supplier of Val medical supplies.
 
---
 
## The Red Mediatoress
- **What it is:** A cosmic being that speaks through Priya's own lips. It addresses Priya as much as it addresses the people in the room, because in a sense Priya is part of it. Asked whether it is a hero, it answers that it is neither *Dharmatman* nor *Papatman*, neither *deva* nor *asura*, nearer to *Brahman*, then corrects itself to "a cosmic being."
- **Manifestation:** A red aura that erupts when Priya is in extreme distress, and her skin turns red while she is the Red Mediatoress. In the first outburst it burned the purple *bhuta* haze off Arjun and Dadi in one shockwave.
- **Offense (shown once):** Casually attempted to erase Bog from existence by making his body thin and transparent. It stopped when Priya caught her own wrist and begged.
- **Attitude:** Treats the lives of ordinary people as insignificant, but respects the will of its own incarnation. Ordered Priya to keep it secret.
- **Mission:** Locate and bury the **singularity** again. The user's beats place it somewhere to the **west**; Priya reads this as the old Mississippi-Alabama border.
### What the Mediatoress has explained
- Earth's "transcendent ones" used *siddhis* and *astras* against the Val, and the singularity buried beneath this place was exposed, so the fabric of the world began to unravel.
- A recent conflict in the **southern seas**, also fought with *siddhis*, was the final blow.
- From the singularity a **"threshold"** has begun to widen. The **bhutas** (malevolent spirits of unburied war dead) are only the first leak. The collapse of the cosmos will follow.
### The vision
When Priya touched Dadi's wrist she saw the bhutas spread from house to house, possess animals, and take the whole world, ending in mutual slaughter and total extinction. This is the moment the red aura erupted.

## Relationships
- **Bog:** Initially the large, rude patient and generous supply donor. Now her escort and **subordinate** by compromise with the Mediatoress. He knows her secret. Priya trusts him in a limited way because of the medical supplies, and Bog, who does not understand the situation, treats it as "a lot of paperwork."
- **Arjun:** Her husband, who wanted to come west with her. She asked him to stay with the family and the clinic.
  - **How they met (draft, not yet confirmed by the author):** Both were in Atlanta, she at Emory studying hero medicine and he at Georgia Tech building medical devices. They met when his lab needed a clinician who understood superhuman patients to test a prototype. He was fascinated by what she described and she was charmed by his habit of testing everything himself first.
- **Dadi:** Hostile to Bog; threatened to find him if Priya is hurt.
- **Dr. Hermes:** Her colleague at the clinic. Killed by a black bear last month (the first of the bhuta-possessed attacks).
## Current Status
Traveling west on foot with Bog toward the old Mississippi-Alabama border to find and bury the singularity. She must keep the Red Mediatoress secret from everyone. Her family remains in Florence.
