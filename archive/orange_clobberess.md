# Character Profile: Yan Rin (Orange Clobberess)
 
## Baseline Identity
- **Hero Name:** Orange Clobberess (nobody uses it; she is simply "Rin")
- **Real Name:** Yan Rin
- **Ancestry:** Chinese descent; a war orphan of unknown nationality
- **Age:** 20
- **Family:** None known (war orphan)
- **Affiliation:** Old Guards, Appalachian branch. Sidekick and protégée of **Darkwing**
- **Powers:** Unknown whether she has any superpowers. She fights inside a massive powered suit.
- **Watch list:** She took no part in heroic activity during the war, so she does not appear on the Val Dominion's watch list.
- **Personality (core):** Cheerful, loud, giggly, and fearless. She is quick to laugh in the middle of a fight. She does a gruff impression of Darkwing's voice to quote his grumbles.
### Appearance
Young East Asian woman with oversized goggles pushed up into a lopsided ponytail.
 
## The Powered Suit
- A roughly nine-meter salvaged suit of mismatched plating, with a school-bus door welded over one shoulder and a cartoon cat sticker on the chest. The cockpit sits behind glass in the middle of the chest.
- Immense strength. It can throw wrecked vehicles, and can slam a Val soldier into the ground until he stops moving.
- It is not orange. She would like to paint it orange, but has no paint; Darkwing points out it would make her "stand out like an idiot in the middle of the woods."
- She has a sketch, drawn by herself, of what the finished, cool-looking orange suit will eventually be.
## Catchphrase
"It's clobberin' time!" is lifted directly from one of her favorite comic books. Its main effect is to make Darkwing grimace. "Clobberess" itself, per Darkwing, "isn't even grammatically correct."
 
## Relationships
- **Darkwing:** mentor and commander. Their dynamic is her exuberance against his dry patience. He called her "very good" and "loud."
- **Nia Okonkwo (Black Huntress):** Rin is assigned as Nia's escort to Dr. Hermes at the Free Clinic's Appalachian branch.
