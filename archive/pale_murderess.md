# Character Profile: Sophia Ivanova (Pale Murderess)
 
## Baseline Identity
- **Villain Name:** Pale Murderess
- **Real Name:** Sophia Ivanova (called "Sonya" by associates)
- **Nationality:** Russian
- **Age:** 29
- **Family:** None established
- **Occupation (pre-defection):** Operator, Russian Federal Security Service (FSB), Alpha Special Operations Unit
- **Powers:** Superhuman strength, durability, speed, agility, reflexes, and senses; dimensional portals (see below)
- **Personality (core):** Laconic, cold, and composed. Soft-spoken and even-toned. Pragmatic and lethal, with no loyalty to heroes or villains alike.
### Appearance
Tall, sturdy white woman with Slavic features and silver hair in an asymmetrical undercut. Pale gray, nearly colorless eyes and a thin white scar through one eyebrow. Wears a long coat. Roughly the same height as Nia Okonkwo.
 
### History
A former FSB Alpha operative who defected after murdering her superior officer, then became a supervillain. Her elite special-forces training in firearms, hand-to-hand combat, infiltration, assassination, reconnaissance, and tactics makes her extremely dangerous even without her powers. Heroes of the Earth Accord regard her as a feared assassin.
 
---
 
## Powers and Abilities
 
### Dimensional Portals
She creates glowing, disc-shaped portals connecting the physical world to another dimension. She freely designates which portal is the entrance and which is the exit; anything entering one (matter, energy, or any physical phenomenon) can emerge from another of her choosing, in a direction she controls.
 
- **Mobility:** Short-range teleportation for herself or objects; passing through walls, floors, and ceilings; instant movement between elevations; redirecting herself to avoid falls.
- **Offense:** Ambushes from behind or beside an enemy; redirecting attacks onto other targets.
- **Defense:** Capturing bullets, missiles, and energy beams and sending them back at the attacker or elsewhere. The portals transfer attacks through space rather than bouncing them.
### Selective Transference
She decides what each portal lets through or blocks, including radio waves and radar, visible light and other radiation, magnetic fields, bullets, objects, and energy attacks.
 
### Spherical Portal Field (Stealth and Defense)
She can surround herself with portals in a sphere. Incoming waves enter the portals on the near side and exit the opposite side, bypassing her, so radar and optical detection pass around her. This is spatial redirection through another dimension, not conventional invisibility. The same field intercepts and redirects physical and energy attacks.
 
### Limitations
- **Range:** Portals can only be opened in her immediate surroundings, so no long-distance teleportation.
- **Size:** Each portal has a maximum diameter; larger ones demand more mental effort.
- **Number:** Only a limited number can be sustained at once; more portals mean more strain.
- **Fatigue:** Portal size and count depend on her stamina, concentration, and physical condition. Exhaustion can collapse active portals. A large spherical field is especially demanding and cannot be held indefinitely.
---
 
## Status During the Occupation (Before the Depot Raid)
 
During the Val invasion she used her portal abilities, particularly the spherical field that evades radar and optical detection, to survive and avoid the Dominion's attention. She did not take part in the defensive efforts of the human coalition and was never captured or fitted with a null-collar, so she retained her full powers. She went into hiding after the invasion.
 
She was counted among the hidden former supervillains who survived the occupation by abandoning public identities and letting the Val believe they were dead.
 
---
 
## Current Status (After the Orbital Depot Raid)
 
During a raid on a Val orbital military depot she turned on her partners (Bog and Nia) and was overpowered by the depot's "space tentacle monster," which Bog took control of. She has been **captured and fitted with a null-collar**, which suppresses her portal abilities. Her superhuman strength and endurance are not her main asset in captivity, and she cannot use her powers while collared.
 
She is held by the tentacle monster in an underground burrow near the former Mobile site. Bog, her captor and owner by way of the monster, ordered her taming and indoctrination.

Scoreboard shows Nia ahead (gap nine at last count). She wants first place in hybrid output, quality of service and Bog's affection, which her competitiveness feeds.

These are logged as a training manual for Bog. She responds positively to a hologram of Nia performing a twerk (she calls it "tactical analysis") and negatively to verbal commentary during it, so recordings are played without commentary and comments are reserved for reward phases. Areas she prefers that Bog's body cannot reach are scheduled as rewards for module completion.

### Personality Under Current Conditions
She still answers everything in Russian profanity, and the monster keeps a log of her vocabulary (334 unique words so far; the log counts numerals and non-Russian curses she delivers with emphasis, ignores duplicates, and logs denials as "cough" or "tactical analysis"). Internally she holds on to the line "Не сдамся" ("I won't surrender"), though she no longer knows whether it is true. Her insults target Bog (cucumber, boiled-potato brains) and the tentacles (temperature complaints); she once cursed his "mother" eleven times before learning Val have no mothers, then redirected the curse to his cradle, and was briefly pleased when told the Quiet Blight was already at work.
