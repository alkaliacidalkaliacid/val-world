### Sonya's powers (as shown in combat)
Discs/portals: entrance/exit chosen freely, selective transference (sound, light, radar, objects, energy). Uses: ambush, redirect attacks, sever objects by snapping a portal shut, drop objects onto targets, mirror-discs around her face to remove blind spots, sound-muffling discs on Nia's bells, stealth sphere. Limits: needs her own surroundings, strain with number/size (four was her usual ceiling, six at the extreme), fatigue (sweat, nosebleed, trembling hand). Also superhuman strength (punch sends weapons 10 m), ordinary bullets don't hurt. Portals cannot find a "core" in the tentacle monster.
 
### The tentacle monster
Ancient autonomous Dominion weapon modeled on a real space beast, a cocoon of steel cables found in the depot's Sector Delta. Regenerates instantly, has no core, absorbs floor plating, weapons and supplies to grow. Targets the strongest non-Val in range; "legend" says it won't attack Val, but it does hit them when clumsy. Bog clamped a spare collar onto a tentacle and the collar acted as a **"General Control System"**: the monster now obeys Bog as "Commander", takes orders literally (asks to "define" vague terms), can talk through collars, can shape tentacles into lifelike forms, can synthesize composition from samples. Humor beat: "In about one Carbon-11 half-life" = about twenty minutes.
