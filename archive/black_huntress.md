# Character Profile: Nia Okonkwo (Black Huntress)

## Baseline Identity

- **Nationality:** Nigerian-American

### Appearance (Pre-Taming)
Tall, muscular, rugged Black woman with a powerful build, close-cropped natural hair, and a scar running from her left temple to her cheek. Commanding presence even in captivity.

### History
Awakened as “Dike Nwanyi” in her youth while rescuing a child from an apartment complex fire. Developed immense physical strength and other powers, resolving numerous crises. During the alien invasion, helped hold the evacuation corridor in Baltimore before being defeated and captured by the Val.
