## Summary (40-60 words)
Bog sees her, grins, and improvises: her pod came down by the Mississippi, his jetpack overshot to Alabama, he walked — her four days of mourning explained as a missed bus. Nia crosses the gravel, bells ringing, stops in his shadow, and tells him she has one thing to say, and then she will decide what to do with her hands.
