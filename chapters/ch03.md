## Summary (40-60 words)
Bog asks for a woman who will not break under a Val's strength. The merchant shows him a secondhand Bound: the Black Huntress, who wiped out a tank division and stays stubborn from loyalty to her mate and cubs. Bog pays. When her field drops she knees him in the face. He shrugs - sturdy - and drives her home.
