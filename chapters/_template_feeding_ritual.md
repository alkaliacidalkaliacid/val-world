## The sequence (all steps, in order)

0. **Position.** She folds down: forehead to the floor (or ground), back
   bared — prostration. The bells go first: they sound as she folds.
1. **The petition.** Recited in archaic / old Val, ending with the glottal
   catch before the closing phrase. She *asks* — the plea includes
   pleading for his blessing (user addendum, 2026-11-20). Nia's form is
   perfect and she tacks on one short, sharp line of her own at the end —
   the only part of the liturgy that is hers; Bog accepts it and praises
   her pronunciation. Rin parrots it mangled (kazoo to an organ) or, alone
   with him, pleads small and unstudied in English: "Please... please,
   master♥."
2. **The toe kisses.** A kiss pressed to each of Bog's green toes, one by
   one, because the liturgy asks for it — *chu♥* per toe. Shown in ch010;
   in ch095 Rin finds them by touch in the dark, bangs falling in her face.
3. **The climb begins.** On her knees, hands braced on calves like tree
   trunks; on the way up she may be given his fingers to lick clean
   (ch092: two fingers, each so thick her tongue has to travel around it).
4. **The testicles.** Reverent kisses showered on them, one press of the
   lips at a time — *chu, chu♥* — unhurried as a rosary (ch095). They are
   enormous, heavy, green: each a warm dense weight that fills the side of
   her cheek; the skin finer here, softer, sliding a little over what it
   holds; taste of salt and clean sweat. Her hands cup underneath,
   holding, the way you hold something in church with both hands because
   one does not feel like enough respect.
5. **Up the shaft, in stations.** She kisses her way up — lips parted,
   tongue flat and tasting — *chu♥, chu* — including kisses along the
   **sides** of the shaft (the route the complaints travel in ch120: she
   does not only suck the tip while talking; she kisses the sides between
   lines). Scale, every time: thicker than her wrist, thicker than any
   tool grip; warm as something with its own weather; a faint ridging the
   tongue reads like print for the blind; a vein running the length of it
   fat as a finger, pulsing slow against her lips.
6. **The mouth, under instruction.** The meal proper, directed in Bog's
   patient teaching register: *Hold. Now move. Slowly. Hold still —
   open. Show me.* Capacity rules: Rin takes the tip only ("Just the
   tip. We will work up to it" — her jaw cannot take more; the hinge
   aches, eyes water, teeth may graze and he does not flinch). Nia takes
   him deep, to the back of the throat. Wet sounds are written out:
   *jupo♥ — jururu — chu♥, chu, nnn♥*; drool escapes at the corners and
   is not always wiped; a shining string stretches from lip to tip when
   she lets go. Bog's hand rests on the back of her head throughout —
   **not pressing, never pressing, just stroking**, slow, the way you
   stroke a cat that has come in out of the rain.
7. **The swallow — only on permission.** "Good girl. You can swallow."
   She swallows; the weight settles in her stomach.
8. **The thanksgiving prayer.** In the old Val tongue, to the one who
   provided the meal: perfect cadence, the glottal catch, the hushed,
   reverent drop at the end. Form detail (ch010): she presses her arms
   against her sides to offer it. Bog praises the performance — "Clever
   girl. So devoted." / "Perfect. Not a single mistake." — and does not
   appear to hear what the words mean to her.

## Standing details (do not drop)

- **Frequency / nature:** three to five times a day in Bog's household;
  it is a *meal* — it heals wounds and brings strength back ("You are
  leaking, and you are empty... First the one, then the other").
- **Bells:** hers chime faintly with every movement throughout — the
  liturgy is accompanied by music whether anyone wants it or not.
- **Her interior counterpoint** runs under the form: Nia counts (412,
  413...) and recites her own litany — *Tomatoes. Scotch bonnet. Thyme.
  Amara. Chidi. Daniel. Hold the line.* — where a good Christian woman
  would put an Amen, she has started putting *him*. Rin thinks in
  runaway spirals (smell, machinery, the Boss, Nia) and may run off the
  end of the steps entirely.
- **Bog's register:** patient instructor and delighted audience, never
  hurried ("Slowly. There is no bell."), praise as punctuation. When she
  overruns the form with appetite: "Easy. Easy, easy." / "Quite the
  glutton..." — the stroking slows, the way you settle a horse.
- ♥ attaches directly to the preceding word (style rule), and hers ride
  on kisses, pleas and mouth-sounds alike.

## Variation knobs

- **Performer:** Nia (flawless form, fury or devotion carried in
  precision; can take him deep; adds her own line to the petition) /
  Rin (clumsy, tip only, jaw ache, improvisation and stampede; goggles,
  bangs, wrist-sweeps) / Priya (no full performance on record yet).
- **Mood:** reverent / furious (ch120: the liturgy administered as a
  scolding, reproaches surfacing between stations) / reluctant /
  greedy / drowsy-tender (ch095's close: cheek laid along the shaft like
  a pillow, a tiny mortifying burp — *gep♥*).
- **Setting pressure:** kitchen routine (ch007), cave dark (ch092, ch095),
  open air with an audience in earshot (ch120).
- **Interruptions:** dialogue, charges, negotiations can be woven
  between stations 4–6; she surfaces for air to speak, kisses the sides
  while gathering the next line, and goes back down.
