## Scene Breakdown (3-5 scenes)
1. At the seam. The two women at the lip of the black seam, the cold air breathing up, the descent about to begin as planned. A small beat of Priya-as-herself - the nurse, resolute - before anything changes.
2. The awakening. Without distress, without warning: the red comes. Skin flushing red, the aura, the sari burning away into the crimson-and-black costume, the sash streaming in a wind that is not there, eyes burning. Nia understands what she is looking at only as it completes - this is the thing that speaks through Priya, and Priya is no longer the one standing there.
3. The reproach. The Mediatoress addresses Nia by category - Dharmatman, Rsi - and holds her to account for a new Astra loosed by the one under her authority. Abstract, brief, cold. Nia cannot follow the charge to its referent (Rin, the blast, ch81) and the text does not follow it for the reader either; what lands is the fact of being blamed, and by what.
4. The declaration. Colder still, and no longer clearly addressed to anyone: humanity is beyond saving, a calamity that widens the tears in the fabric of the universe; when the burying of the singularity is complete, a resolution will be necessary. Whether she speaks to the woman whose body she wears, or only to herself, is left unresolved.
5. The descent. She rises into the air - and drops, straight down into the depths of the earth, leaving Nia behind on the mountainside. End on Nia alone at the seam.
