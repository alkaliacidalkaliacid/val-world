## Summary (40-60 words)
The suit falls into the mountain and lands, buoyed by the purple, in a lightless cavern. Rin survives — the cockpit was built sturdiest, the Boss's doctrine — but the suit will not move and the quiet goes on too long. A voice echoes down the shaft: 'Hey there!' Bog comes bounding down the wall. '...Yes,' says Rin. 'Please. Yes.'

## Facts Established in This Chapter
- The suit falls into the mountain, buoyed and slowed by the purple aura ("somewhat like being *held*"), and lands in a vast, lightless cavern lit only by violet seeping from the stone. Rin is bruised all over but unbroken — the cockpit, built to be the sturdiest part under Darkwing's design doctrine (the outer shell breaks away to dissipate impact), held.
- The suit is dead here: levers, pedals, the little red button she was never allowed to press, ten minutes with a wrench in the engine bay — nothing moves it. No exit, rope, ladder or daylight is visible; the air is thick, "as if someone had put a lid on the world".
- Rin does not cry — a decision she made at fourteen, when the sky was on fire, because crying takes too much air. What wears her down is the quiet: the Boss or Nia has always come by now.
- Bog comes bounding down the shaft, riding the rising purple streams, weightless and delighted ("it makes you feel weightless! Like being a feather!"), and invites her to join him. Ashamed of her own relief — there must be a rule about not being delighted to see a Val — she accepts: "...Yes. Please. Yes."
