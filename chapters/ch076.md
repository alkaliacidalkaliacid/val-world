## Summary (40-60 words)
Bog is alive. Nia watches him flatten a giant with the laser, then throw it into the ravine — and shakes apart in the cockpit. He ignores her, hoists a woman in black-and-crimson onto his shoulders — 'Boss, when you're ready' — and the woman pours crimson light that makes the purple scream and recoil, and every bell on Nia's body goes silent.
