## Facts Established in This Chapter
- The possessed suit outclasses Bog completely — throws him wall to wall, bursts his nose (green blood), creaks his ribs. The cockpit glass is exposed: one fist through it would end the fight, and Bog will not do it — "like being asked to eat a puppy". The text insists it is temperament, not nobility.
- Bog's kit audit: the laser is gone (thrown into the ravine, ch076), the rolling pin was left in Florence (ch061); what he carries is one of the two null collars lifted from Hermes's lab (ch069), on his belt.
- He drops flat, slides under the suit's swinging arms, tears the cockpit glass away and snaps the collar around Rin's throat. The suit folds and goes dark — but Rin comes out of the cockpit still possessed, clawing, and the collar alone is losing against "a hundred thousand years of dead things"; her body is starting to buckle under the contest.
- A half-remembered Dominion handler's manual gives the emergency method: *in cases of collar instability, bond-calm by direct contact* — a footnote, involving a diagram. Bog pins her gently (one hand cupping her head off the stone), apologises ("Sorry about this. It's for your health"), and kisses her, counting to five. The collar flares white; the purple screams out of her mouth, nose, ears and the corners of her eyes, and flees into the dark.
- Rin wakes herself — brown eyes, bewildered, a thread of Bog's blood across her cheek: "...Lucky Val? Did you just kiss me?" She now wears a physical null-collar.
