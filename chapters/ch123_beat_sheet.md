## Scene Breakdown
1. **Arrival and the suits.** The monster reaches the outskirts of
   Florence and hides in the woods. Nia points out that Priya and Rin
   need clothing; the monster provides Val armoured spacesuits from
   the depot stock it carries — heavy, and far too large for either
   of them. The party enters Florence to stares: Bog; a tall, robust,
   dark-skinned woman in overalls; and Priya and the Asian girl
   struggling to walk in the oversized armour.
2. **The reunion.** Word of Priya's return runs ahead of them. Arjun
   rushes out of his workshop, ignores her bizarre attire entirely —
   true to his character — and pulls her into an embrace. The
   daughters come running to her too.
3. **The glare.** "Good for them," Bog says. Rin nods vigorously.
   Nia shoots Bog a sharp glare.
4. **The meal.** Everyone is invited to Priya's home for a meal.
   Priya explains that she has completed her mission, leaving out
   the specific details. During the gathering Bog casually drapes an
   arm around Nia's shoulder and pulls her close; Nia grinds her
   teeth — still better than having her breasts or backside groped.
   Watching, Dadi's stern expression softens: she has concluded that
   Nia is Bog's wife. The public display of affection is a bit much,
   but perhaps Bog is not such a bad man after all — though,
   unfortunately, that is far from the truth.
5. **The daughters and Rin.** Meera and Kavya are fascinated by Rin,
   who is closer to them in age, and pepper her with questions:
   "What's it like in Mississippi?" "Can you do kung fu?" Rin
   answers with enthusiasm — and says nothing about the Old Guards.
6. **Coda (narrator aside).** Did we forget anyone? Oh, don't worry —
   Sonya is doing just fine. She has just reached her 31st
   consecutive orgasm with the monster.
