## Summary (40-60 words)
Bog finds fossils in the walls — epochs of dead things in the stone. The purple assembles them into a three-skulled monster; he kicks it apart like a dog, but the aura lifts off, drifts toward Rin's wrench, and pours into the suit. It stands, violet, her eyes blazing in the glass. 'It's clobberin' time!' it says, and charges.

## Facts Established in This Chapter
- The cavern walls hold fossils: ribs like a half-built ship's timbers, vertebrae the size of barrels, a long low skull with a double row of teeth — herds and whole epochs of creatures from when the world was wet and green, "laid out in the stone like a library".
- The purple settles on the bones and assembles them into a single composite creature — a hundred legs, three skulls. Bog kicks it apart with one casual kick, but the aura is unharmed: it lifts off the scattered bones and drifts across the cavern toward the sound of Rin's wrench.
- The aura pours into the dead suit through its seams. The suit stands, glowing violet from every seam and rivet, powered by the possession rather than fuel; behind the cockpit glass, Rin's eyes blaze wide open with the same fire. In her voice, doubled and tripled, it speaks her catchphrase — "It's clobberin' time!" — and charges Bog.
