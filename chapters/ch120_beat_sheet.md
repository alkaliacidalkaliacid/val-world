## Scene Breakdown (2 scenes)
1. The interception. Bog, turning back from the hatch toward his three
   consorts, meets Nia's eyes — she is coming for him, and her eyes are
   burning with rage. He reads the trouble correctly and walks into it
   anyway: crosses to her, pulls her into an embrace, and showers the
   affection he cannot help while she struggles against his hold. "We
   need to talk." "Alright, alright" — placating, unbothered — he leads
   her toward the shade of a tree near the monster, and the monster,
   tactless as a hotel clerk with a loudspeaker, announces: "Caution:
   The sonic repellent field for the consorts is set to a 30-meter
   radius, Commander."
2. The tribunal at the log. Nia sits Bog down on a fallen log and,
   still fuming, begins the feeding ritual — deliberate, lingering,
   infused with deep affection and lingering frustration both. She takes
   her long-awaited feast at last, receiving the head-strokes and pats
   she has been craving, and between slurps and ♥s she lays her charges:
   (a) Rin — "She's only twenty years old!" Bog: "And you're only
   thirty-nine, aren't you?" (b) Priya — "You've preyed on another
   married mother!" Bog: it was necessary — to get another diva to do as
   she was told. An explanation that only provokes her further.
   (c) Herself — the charge that costs her most: he fed Priya, he fed the
   girl, and she lay there and watched, craving, the primary consort fed
   last. Bog answers with strokes and placation, no denial. The storm
   spends itself into the liturgy's close: hold, show, permission asked
   and given, the swallow, the prayer of thanks — hoarse, furious and
   sincere at once. She stays kneeling, forehead against his thigh:
   they are not finished. "No," Bog agrees, stroking her hair.
   "Alright."
