## Summary (40-60 words)
Bog, a Val soldier on leave, strolls a black market in a ruined car park: diverted Dominion supplies, caged alien creatures, and captive women greeting every Val with a raised middle finger. Between a doomsday preacher and a Val-worshipping cultist, he considers buying a sturdy slave for chores and breeding, puts it off, and buys a grilled giant-cockroach skewer instead.

## Facts Established in This Chapter
- Bog likes Earth and is thinking of settling; Val comrades buy slaves for chores and eventual breeding, under a sworn oath to keep them healthy.
- The Elites forbid the market's trade; female captives are protected and well fed under Elite policy.
- (Added in the 2026-11-17 review pass, per the user's rulings) Most women in the market row are ordinary captives; the only Bound there is one collared woman (unnamed in the text; Nia).
