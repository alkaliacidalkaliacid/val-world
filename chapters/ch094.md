# Chapter Summary: ch094 Resolution

> New writing, 2026-11-18. Beat sheet: chapters/ch094_beat_sheet.md. Prose: manuscript/ch094_resolution.md (980 words).

## Summary (40-60 words)
At the seam, Priya's recovery lets the Red Mediatoress manifest without distress: costume, red skin, burning eyes. She reproaches Nia — Dharmatman, Rsi — for a new astra loosed by the one beneath her hand, and declares humanity a calamity widening the universe's tears: after the burying, a resolution will be necessary. She drops into the earth. Nia climbs down alone.

## Facts Established in This Chapter
- The Red Mediatoress can manifest when Priya has recovered enough strength; distress is not required. The manifestation follows the established pattern: red flush over the skin, red aura, the sari charring away into the crimson-and-black costume (gold waist band, wide red sash streaming in a wind that touches nothing else), eyes burning red. The mechanism is not explained in the text.
- The Mediatoress addresses Nia by her own taxonomy — "Dharmatman," "Rsi" — and holds her accountable for a new "astra" loosed by "the one who stands beneath your hand": Rin's suit blast against the dead (ch081), though the text does not spell out the referent and Nia only half-guesses it. "Ignorance is not an account. See that it is not offered twice."
- The Mediatoress's verdict, delivered coldly and possibly not addressed to Nia at all (she may be speaking to Priya, or to herself): humanity widens the tears in the fabric of the universe and calls it survival; there is no saving it; it is the calamity it fears. When the burying of the singularity is finished and the threshold shut, "a resolution will be necessary." The term is not explained.
- The Mediatoress rises above the seam and drops straight down into the earth, leaving Nia behind — the joint descent planned in ch093 is overturned, exactly as foreshadowed.
- Nia, alone, begins climbing down the seam by herself, after Bog, Rin, and whatever has gone down wearing Priya.
