## Synopsis (200-250 words max)
Bog, a low-ranking Val soldier and reservist, wants to settle on occupied Earth. Ordered to obtain a breeding consort within three days or be posted to Tau Ceti, he buys the captive superhero Nia Okonkwo, the Black Huntress, at a slave market. Their secret bargain: she will live as his devoted consort; in return he will remove her null-collar and let her search for her lost husband and children. Bog joins a raid on an orbital depot to steal a collar-removal toolkit. The raid unleashes an ancient tentacle monster, which, fitted with a spare collar, accepts Bog as its commander and captures and collars the supervillain Sonya, now held in its burrow. Separated in the escape, Bog and Nia journey toward each other: Nia, believing Bog dead, travels with the hero Rin toward Dr. Hermes, unaware he is dead, while Bog escorts Priya, a doctor possessed by the Red Mediatoress, toward a buried singularity. On a mountain crowned by purple light the journeys converge, and Bog and Nia are reunited. But Priya's burying of the singularity stalls unfinished; its backlash takes Rin's suit, then Rin herself. Bog dives into the rift, frees her with a Hermes collar and declares her his third consort - confiding the plan he still hides from Nia: she will leave her husband, become his wife properly, and bear him many children. Above them, Priya wakes under Nia's guard, and it is Nia who opens the talk.

## Major Turning Points (max 7)
1. Bog buys Nia at the slave market; she becomes his captive "consort."
2. The secret deal: devoted service in exchange for collar removal and a family search.
3. The orbital depot raid; the tentacle monster awakens and accepts Bog as commander.
4. Sonya is defeated, collared, and held by the monster for taming.
5. Nia escapes with the Class 5 toolkit, is captured by Val, and is rescued by the Old Guards (Darkwing and Rin).
6. Dr. Hermes is revealed to be dead; Priya awakens as the vessel of the Red Mediatoress and recruits Bog as her escort west.
7. The journeys converge on the mountain: Bog and Nia are reunited, the singularity is only partly buried, and Rin is collared and made third consort.

## Where We Are Now (max 3 lines)
- Current arc: Convergence on the mountain - Bog, Nia, Priya and Rin are all at the mountain with the purple pillar; Sonya remains in the burrow.
- Protagonist's current goal: Bog - protect the exhausted Priya as the Mediatoress ordered, and still free Nia under the deal, while keeping his secrets from her.
- Biggest crisis / mystery: The singularity is only partly buried and its backlash, driven out of Rin, is still loose; Nia's talk with Priya is about to happen, and Bog's plan is still secret from her.
