## Chapter 2: The Ultimatum


Grilled cockroach skewers, it turned out, tasted pretty good. Smoky, a little sweet, with a crunch like fried shrimp. Bog was licking the last of the glaze off his thumb when the communicator on his forearm began to flash.

It was his platoon leader.

"Enjoying your leave, Bog?" The voice crackled through the speaker, as gravelly as ever. "If you want to stay on Earth and join the reserves, you've got three days to buy yourself a breeding consort and do your duty to the Dominion. Otherwise, the military police will drag you in and ship you off to the Tau Ceti system. Nothing there but one giant bog of a planet. The Elites say the locals evolved from brittle stars, and, get this, they're supposedly compatible with us."

The line went dead.

Bog stood very still, the empty skewer still in his hand. Behind him, the preacher and the cultist were shouting past each other again.

"A bog," he muttered. "Of course it's a bog."
