## Chapter 9: Eco-Friendly


Bog pulled back from the kiss, a little flushed, his tusked grin sheepish. "I've got a favor to ask," he said. "From now on, every night, I want you to wash me. With your strong, beautiful body, like a sponge. Plenty of soap, of course." He nodded, pleased with himself. "And if we shower together, we save water. It's eco-friendly, right?"

Nia stared at him.

"Eco-friendly," she repeated.

"I read it in a magazine. Earth magazine, from before." He sounded proud. "They said everyone should conserve water."

*There is a Val water-processor humming in the basement of this house. I've seen it. It could fill a lake. His kind came close to turning entire oceans into fuel and he's worried about the water bill.*

*He doesn't even believe it. He just likes the way it sounds.*

*Look at his face. A green giant, blushing, quoting an advice column to a woman he owns. He thinks he's being charming. He's trying to flirt in the language of a dead world, and he's using the worst phrase in it.*

"You're an idiot," she said.

"That's a yes?"

"It's an observation."

"I'll take it." He beamed.

The shower was a stone cubicle at the back of the house, big enough for him and cramped for two. The bells clinked against the tile as the water came on. He handed her the soap, a bar of that sharp lavender, and then stood very still and let her work.

*Reconnaissance,* she reminded herself, soaping her palms. *Know the terrain.*

*Shoulders, wide as a doorway. Ribs under thick muscle. A long, jagged scar from collarbone to hip that none of the regeneration treatments has touched. Whatever gave him that, it nearly killed him, and he wears it like an old coat.*

*He's shivering. Under my hands. A thing that could survive even after being crushed by a tank is shivering because a woman is washing his back.*

*Don't soften. Memorize. The scar. The heartbeat. The place under the left shoulder blade where the plating thins.*

"You're very good at this," Bog murmured, eyes closed, water running down his blunt, happy face.

"Shut up, pickle."

"Yes, ma'am."

*Amara. Chidi. Daniel.*

*Eco-friendly.*

She rolled her eyes at the ceiling, and, for one terrible second, nearly laughed.

---

Steam filled the stone cubicle until the walls ran with it. Lather covered both of them, thick and white and smelling of lavender, and Nia's dark, slick body was pressed full-length against a chest like a green cliff face. They moved together in a slow, rolling rhythm, soap squeaking, the bells at her chest and hips chiming softly against his skin with every pass.

He kissed her more here than he had in the other room. Warm water poured over both of them, and he kissed her through it, her mouth, her jaw, her wet shoulder, one after another, as though the shower had given him permission.

"Hmm," Bog rumbled against her ear. "You never tried this with your former consort either? Isn't that a bit cruel to him?"

His hands drifted down to her backside and began to knead, big and thorough and entirely unembarrassed.

*Cruel. To* him.

Nia reached back and swatted them away with a crack that echoed off the tile. "Stay *still,*" she snapped. "You asked to be washed. So hold still and let me wash you, or I'll do it with the scrub brush."

"Yes, ma'am." He put his hands on the wall, meek as a schoolboy, and let her work.

*Good. Keep your head. This is a job.*

She ran her soapy palms along his ribs and shoulders, and tried not to notice how heavy and warm and ridiculously solid he was. Muscle on top of muscle, a body built to a scale that had no business sharing a shower with a human. When she leaned in to reach his back, her cheek landed against his chest, and she could feel the slow, enormous drum of his heart.

*Frustrating. That's the word. Frustratingly rugged, frustratingly strong, frustratingly warm, like standing against a furnace in a freezing house.*

*Daniel was lean. Daniel was soft around the middle and apologized for it, and I'd tease him, and he'd laugh. His arms fit around me. They didn't swallow me.*

*Don't compare. It's not a contest. It's a mission.*

*But the body doesn't read mission statements. The body just says warm, and safe, and clean, and held, in a voice that's very hard to argue with.*

Her fingers slowed. She caught herself, scowled, and went back to scrubbing with more force than the job required.

"Ow," Bog said mildly.

"Quiet."

"Yes, ma'am."

*Amara. Chidi. Daniel.*

*He's not Daniel. He is not Daniel. He is the worst thing that ever happened to me.*

*So why is it so hard to remember that when his heart is that slow?*

The water ran. The bells chimed. Somewhere under the lather, her hands were shaking again.
