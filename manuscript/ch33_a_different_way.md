## Chapter 33: A Different Way


He frowned. He had the oddest sense that he had just been outvoted by himself.

Another tremor rolled through the creature, and a thin scream of cable rose from somewhere below. He cleared his throat and looked up at the collar, which was still glowing a faint pulsing blue.

"Hey. When you say 'neutralize,' what do you mean, exactly?"

"To cease the functions of the life form," the monster said.

"So, kill her," Bog muttered.

"Affirmative."

"Right. Right." He rubbed his chin with his good hand, and the silence stretched out around them, filled with the groan of metal and the roar of air against the hull. The orange light from the viewport flickered across his face. "Hmm."

Then he began to grin.

It started small, at one corner of his wide, blunt mouth, and spread until his sharp teeth showed in the red glow. It was the grin of a man who had just remembered that he was, in fact, a soldier of the Val Dominion, with a heavy weapon at his command and all the time in the world.

"Listen. I'll show you a different way," he said. "A much more entertaining one."

"Understood, Commander."

Far below them, in the gutted heart of the monster, Sonya's portals were still flickering. And the thing that had been trying to kill her grew very quiet.
