## Chapter 107: Something Special

He held her at the waist, hoisted in his arms like something he had caught and meant to keep, and Nia — dangling, breathless, furious — did what she had been storing up to do all the way down the mountain.

"You leapt," she said. "Into a chasm. After her. No rope, no plan, no — no *thought*, Bog, not one single thought in that enormous green head—"

"She was falling," Bog said.

"—and that is not a defence, that is the *charge*—"

"It worked out."

"It always works out! That is the worst thing about you! It works out, and you learn nothing, and next time you leap further! You are reckless, and thoughtless, and stubborn as a gate, and—" She was gathering speed now, weeks of it behind her: the believing him dead, the grieving, the mountain, the dark. "—and a lecher, and a fool, and entirely— mmph."

Bog had considered the defence. There was, he felt, a case to be made. But the case was long, and she was warm, and she was *here*, in his arms, smelling of cold stone and sweat and herself, and the whole thing was simply too much of a hassle.

So he kissed her instead.

She had been denied him a long time. That was the plain arithmetic of it: the separation, the mountain, the collar's patient, grinding hunger that Priya had looked at and called unwellness. The consort's body had been waiting with a single-mindedness her mind had never once consented to — and now her master had her in his arms and his mouth on hers, and the body stopped waiting all at once.

The climax took her from the kiss alone. It seemed the treatment reserved for the primary consort was, indeed, something special.
