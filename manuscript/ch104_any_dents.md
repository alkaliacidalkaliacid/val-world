## Chapter 104: Any Dents?

It ended the way a light ends.

Priya's power faded — not spent in any flash, just thinning, the way the screaming had thinned into one long vowel — and the red light that had filled the cavern guttered with it. Which was a problem for exactly one person.

Rin fell.

She landed on her bottom on the cavern floor with a thump and a yelp, and sat there in the sudden dark rubbing her backside with both hands, eyes welling up, more insulted than injured. The red light vanished entirely. Darkness descended, total and ordinary. Somewhere above the stone, nothing glowed. The Red Mediatoress, it seemed, was no longer active.

"Well," Bog muttered, to the woman still in his arms, "that worked out, I suppose."

He kissed her once more — unnecessarily, lingering over it, a man signing his name to a finished job — and then slid his softened member out of her and lowered her, gently, all that praised and swaying flesh arranged with care, and laid her down on the floor.

A sniffle from the darkness. A small, mortified voice, rubbing as it spoke:

"What if she ends up... um... preg... nant?"

"I'd be happy if that happened," Bog said. "Though I hear it's not quite that simple." His hand found Priya's belly in the dark and stroked it, slow and proprietary, the soft round favourite. He turned his head toward where he sensed Rin to be. "Any dents?"

In the darkness, in the direction of his voice, Rin raised her middle finger.

Then she thought better of it, and switched it for her little finger instead.

Bog felt his way to the wall, leaned back against it, and caught his breath. The cavern settled into its own sounds: water somewhere, stone cooling, Priya's breathing, deep and even. And Rin — shifting, fidgeting, restless in the black, a girl with too much leftover everything and nowhere to put it.

"Come here," Bog said.

He patted his own thigh, twice, a sound with an address on it. In the dark he heard her decide, and then he heard her crawling toward him on all fours across the stone, guided by nothing but the patting and his breathing, the small sounds of knees and palms finding their way.

Before long, a wet, slurping sound began in the darkness.

It had been going a little while when there came a rustling from the floor — Priya, stirring awake, surfacing out of whatever country she had been in. Her voice came back small and private and hers, stammer and all:

"I-I-I want it too...♥"

A slightly dissatisfied grumble from the vicinity of Bog's lap.

But only slightly, and not for long. Soon the slurping sounds from the floor joined the first, and the two settled, adjusted, and harmonised — close, unhurried, companionable — into a duet in the dark.
