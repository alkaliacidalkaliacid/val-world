That lecherous, unprincipled, green invader. ## Chapter 110: Just Do It

For a moment nobody moved. The searchlight held on Bog. The tentacles held on everything else. And Nia's stalagmite, raised and ready, found itself with nothing whatsoever to hit.

Bog looked away.

## Chapter 111: Stealth Mode

The monster took Bog's order the way it took all orders: completely, and at once.

## Chapter 112: General Control System

## Chapter 113: I Wasn't Asked

Bog sat up. The bath was over.

## Chapter 114: Standard Equipment

The questions came from all over the compartment.

## Chapter 115: Loyalty Rating

## Chapter 116: External Control

## Chapter 117: Free to Fly

## Chapter 118: A Little While Longer

## Chapter 119: Forgotten

While they slept, the monster travelled.
