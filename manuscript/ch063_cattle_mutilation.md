## Chapter 63: Cattle Mutilation


Val soldiers, even lazy ones like Bog, are massive and immensely strong. So are bears. Add a *bhuta*, a malevolent spirit with nothing to lose and no body of its own to protect, and it was hard to say who had the upper hand.

A green fist slammed into the bear's snout with a wet crack. The animal answered with a hairy forepaw that could have torn a human face clean off. Bog ducked, and the claws scored four furrows through the plaster where his head had been. The jaws snapped shut on empty air, then again, and then a third time so close that he felt the heat of its breath on his ear. He dropped his chin an instant before the teeth met. A skull-crushing bite closed by a hair's breadth above his scalp.

Another forepaw was already swinging at him.

Priya gasped.

"Priya!" Arjun shouted from across the room. "The Val medical laser! It's in my workshop! Go!"

She stared at him. Then she ran, bare feet slapping through the curry and broken crockery, and vanished down the back hallway. Dadi's voice rose behind her, still praying.

Arjun backed away from the doorway, putting distance between himself and his wife, and began beating the fire poker against a cast-iron pot. The clang filled the house.

"Hey! Hey, over here! Here, bear!"

The bear's head swung toward the noise. Its eyes narrowed on the thin, trembling man with the pot, who was clearly the better meal, and its weight began to shift.

Bog did not like that.

He was bleeding freely from the nose. The fact offended him. He gathered himself, drove upward off the floor with his knees, and slammed his skull into the underside of the creature's jaw. Teeth clacked together with a sound like a dropped crate of plates. The great head snapped back, and a spray of red went up across the ceiling.

"You do NOT," Bog roared, spitting blood, "ignore me when I'm about to hit you! I'm gonna subject you to a *cattle mutilation*!!!"

In the hallway, Priya skidded to a halt. She did not know what a Val cattle mutilation was. She was nearly certain she did not want to.

The bear shook off the blow. Along its spine, the purple aura flared bright, and the glow crept forward over its shoulders and up its neck, as though something were pulling itself out of the animal and into the light. Its mouth opened wider than any bear's mouth should.

What came out was not a roar. It was a voice, thin and many-layered, like a crowd of people speaking from the bottom of a well.

*Not... buried...*

Arjun dropped the pot.

Down the hall, a door banged open, and Priya's voice came back faintly over the noise. "Found it! I-I've got it, I've got the laser!"

Bog wiped his nose on his forearm and grinned.
