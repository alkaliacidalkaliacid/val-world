## Chapter 32: Commander


"A 'general control system'? This collar?"

"Yes, Commander."

"Never heard of it. This thing's supposed to weaken superheroes."

"Please define 'superhero.'"

"Uh… never mind."

Bog took a long, slow breath. It occurred to him that the weapons the Dominion was waving around these days might have been built for entirely different purposes in the first place. He chewed on that for a fleeting moment, then let it go. The Val, and the lower ranks especially, never read the manuals. They never wondered what a piece of equipment had originally been designed for or what philosophy lay behind it. In the field, established custom was all that mattered, and the custom was: *it works, so it works.*

"For now, just follow my orders."

"Yes, Commander."

"Land us safely on Earth. On this planet."

"Yes, Commander."

Outside the cracked viewport, the orange glow had deepened to a furnace red. The pod rattled so hard that Bog's teeth clicked together, but the tentacles that had been crushing the hull had gone slack and now wrapped around it like a cradle. The creature, he realized, was holding them. Somewhere deep in its belly something began to hum, and the tumble slowed, then steadied into a long, stately fall.

"Good. Good. Wonderful." He wiped his face with his sleeve. "Now what about the supervillain? The human woman you were fighting?"

"Engagement is ongoing."

"*Seriously?* We're in freefall!"

"Report, Commander. The hostile activated an emergency charge emplaced to destroy this unit. This unit was not destroyed. She has teleported into an escape pod absorbed by this unit, and maintains a habitable environment within it. She continues her acts of destruction."

Somewhere beneath his feet, something exploded. The whole mass shuddered. A thin howl of tearing cable rose through the walls, followed by the long, wet rip of a limb being cut at the root.

Bog went pale green to the tips of his ears.

"Oh no. Oh no, oh no, oh no. *What do I do?*"

"In approximately one Carbon-11 half-life," the voice said calmly, "we will gain the upper hand and be able to neutralize her."

He looked at the glowing red viewport, at the planet swelling behind it, and at the faint trembling in the floor as another cut tore through the creature's guts. He pressed his good hand to his forehead. "Seriously…"
