## Chapter 24: Betrayal


Using her immense strength, Sonya hauled the supplies she wanted into a single pile, and last of all she set the steel-cable cocoon on top of it.

While she worked, Bog casually picked up a toolkit. Nia gripped his shoulder and whispered, "Are you really going to let her take those weapons? If that broker, or whoever he answers to, gets hold of them, they'll be used for something terrible."

Bog shrugged and whispered back, "Doesn't concern us, does it? If they help the struggle against the Dominion, shouldn't you be all for it?"

"The people those villains will use them on are human beings. Defenseless civilians."

"Civilians? There's barely a 'city' left anymore…"

Nia stared at him. "Do something."

"Wha…? Hey, come on. You should be focusing on your own freedom…" Under her unwavering gaze, Bog faltered. "Well. I suppose the tentacle monster might be a bit much," he muttered, and trudged over to Sonya.

"Hey, you might want to leave that tentacle monster behind. I hear it's completely uncontrollable. The Dominion even tried to get rid of most of them."

Sonya laughed softly. "It seems the hero does not bow to you. You bow to her."

"Look out!" Nia shouted. Bog leaped aside on instinct. A portal flashed open and snapped shut on the very spot where he had been standing a heartbeat earlier.

"Wait… let's talk about this…" But then it dawned on him. The Pale Murderess had meant to dispose of these two nuisances, neatly and quietly, right here in the warehouse. She had meant to from the very beginning.
