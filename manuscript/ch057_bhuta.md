## Chapter 57: Bhuta


Bog flexed his left arm, rolling the shoulder in a slow circle. "Impressive. Running Val medical equipment like that. You're quite the mechanic."

"Oh, n-no." Priya looked a little embarrassed. "I'm not really good with machinery. M-My husband had the knack for that. He used to help Dr. Hermes analyze the Val gear. My own specialty is superhuman medicine. It just so happens that some of it applies to Val as well."

In other words, she was less a nurse than a doctor, perhaps even a PhD. But Bog didn't give it much thought. He *was* Val, after all. He simply said:

"That's amazing."

He fell silent for a moment, then steered the conversation toward less intellectual ground.

"Are black bears dangerous?"

"Yes, especially since the war. There's even a rumor that they're possessed by *bhuta*."

"Whoa. That's wild. What's a *bhuta*?"

Priya swallowed hard and sat down beside him. "Malevolent spirits," she said. "The souls of people who died in the war and were never properly buried, so they linger on the earth."

She spoke with an intensity that seemed oddly out of character for a woman of such an intellectual profession.
