## Chapter 22: Transport


Inside a transport craft, powered by some inscrutable mechanism devised by a mad-scientist-style human supervillain, Sonya, Bog, and "Tummy-Achy Bunny" sat in an awkward silence. Well, perhaps not entirely awkward; Bog, for one, seemed quite content, kneading the thighs of the bunny.

The transport was closing in on the Val military depot, which sat in geostationary orbit.

"How are we going to slip past Val's hyperspace radar?" Nia asked. Bog shot her a look, as if to say, *Hey, that wasn't the deal!* But Nia had already concluded that their cover was blown.

Sonya didn't answer. Instead she countered with a question: "An American hero. Never fought in Eurasia."

"Bunny," Nia replied. The two glared at each other, both refusing to share any information.

Bog sighed. It seemed that, just like in comic books, supervillains and superheroes were destined to be at odds no matter the situation. Still, he decided to look on the bright side: at least the Pale Murderess's costume was quite racy.

The transport's AI announced their approach to the depot. Sonya stood and raised her hand, and a glowing disc opened inside the craft. Without a word she leaped into it, and the other two scrambled after her. For a fleeting moment they were falling through a surreal, incomprehensible landscape — then out the far side of it, into vacuum.

There was no air. Then, all at once, there was: air bled out of the other place through countless tiny discs, streaming around them, thin and freezing and barely there, but it pressed against their skin and pushed them gently onward. The little discs winked open and shut on every side, a tunnel of light — a docking tube made of portals. Nia's ears sang. She kept her mouth shut and held her breath, the way you do underwater. Its last disc opened on the far side of the depot's wall, and before they knew it, they were inside the warehouse.
