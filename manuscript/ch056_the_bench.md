## Chapter 56: The Bench


Bog heaved his jetpack as far as he could throw it, stretched out, and dropped onto an outdoor waiting bench with a groan. A nurse had noticed a faint awkwardness in his left arm's movement — a small thing, the kind only an excellent clinician would catch — and came over with a case of Val medical equipment.

"L-Let me take a look at that."

"Much obliged."

She worked quietly, and after a while she asked in a low voice, "You're the v-volunteer who keeps bringing us medical supplies, aren't you?"

"Business, actually."

A brief silence followed.

"Even so, Dr. Hermes and the rest of us are truly grateful," she said. "You've saved so many lives. Especially the children."

"Well, that's good to hear."

The nurse let out a small, involuntary chuckle at his offhand reply.

"M-My name is Priya."

"Bog. Say, can I ask you something?"

"Of course. Go ahead."

"Has a super-beautiful, muscular Black girl come by here? Dressed like a bunny?"

(The "super-beautiful" was entirely Bog's own assessment, and a woman of Nia's age was hardly a "girl.")

"No, she hasn't."

"I see."

Silence settled over the bench. The treatment was finished.
