## Chapter 34: Sonya's Limit


Sonya was reaching her limit.

Her strength, at least, was still intact. A single punch or kick could send a weapon, Earth-made or Val-made, flying ten yards, and the ordinary handgun rounds the soldiers had fired earlier hadn't so much as scratched her. But her real weapon had been the portals, and against this thing the portals were useless. She could sever the tentacles as they came, but they grew back almost instantly. She could slip through a disc to escape, but she'd only come out in the hollow of the creature's own body, among more cables. There was no heart to cut out, no brain to destroy, no core at all. Her temples throbbed. Her vision had begun to swim at the edges. Blood from her nose had dried to a crust on her lip.

She spat a curse in Russian.

"Hello, hello! Can you hear me, Pale Murderess? It's me, your business partner, Bog."

Sonya froze.

The voice had come from a tentacle. It hung in the air before her, swaying gently, and the sound issued from a gray band clamped around its middle, an awful, cheerful, gravelly voice she had last heard whining about a missing toolkit.

Her face tightened with rage.

"If you agree to a truce, I'll call off the attack."

She rejected the offer outright, with a word that would have made a Val drill sergeant blush.

"Good grief. I figured as much." The tentacle gave an almost human shrug. "Well, I'll have to resort to force, then. Don't worry, I won't kill you. I've decided to have this creature fill a certain role instead. The kind you used to see promised on the covers of old Earth magazines."

*What?*

Sonya blinked. She had never read an old American science-fiction magazine in her life. She did not understand a word of this.

Something cold coiled around her ankle.

She cut it off with a disc. Another tentacle wound itself around her instead. She cut that one too, and a third took its place, and a fourth, and the discs she opened were smaller each time, and slower. Her breath came in hard, shallow bursts. *Don't think. Hold the sphere. Cut. Cut. Cut.* But the limb she'd just severed was already sprouting again, and she couldn't keep this up. Her body was burning through what little she had left.

A tentacle looped around her foot a fifth time. This time she didn't cut it.

She didn't have the strength.

It tightened, almost gently, and the tip slid along the top of her foot, tracing the tendons one by one, light as a feather. Then it did it again.

Sonya's eye twitched.

"What are you…"

The tip drew a slow, careful line down the arch of her foot, and then another across the sole, and then all of them at once.

A sound escaped her. A strangled, high, entirely undignified squeak.

"*Ahh…!*"

Her hand flew to her mouth. She clamped her lips shut. She was a former operative of the FSB's Alpha Group, an assassin who had put a bullet through a general's head at four hundred meters, a woman who had never once laughed in the presence of an enemy. The tentacle wriggled harder. A second one joined it, and a third slid under her coat and found her ribs.

Her whole body convulsed.

"Hahh… no… *nyet*… ha… *stop!*"

"Entertaining?" Bog's voice asked, from the collar.
