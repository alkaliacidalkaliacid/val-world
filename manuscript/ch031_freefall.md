## Chapter 31: Freefall


Bog sat in the dark, wedged between a dead console and a groaning bulkhead, and decided the thing to do was to be calm.

*All right. Let's think this through.*

*Situation: an Earth-side supervillain got into a scuffle with one of the Dominion's space monsters, and now I'm falling toward the planet inside the monster's stomach. Simple enough. So, how do I get out of this alive?*

Nothing came to mind.

*Maybe the Russian witch will have a sudden change of heart and come to save me.* He snorted. *Not that she could. And she hates me, anyway.*

The pod shuddered. Through the cracked viewport, the darkness outside had begun to take on a faint, sickly orange glow. Bog gritted his teeth.

*Right now we're still high up, and falling slow. The heat's barely started. Which means…* He frowned. *Wait. Hold on. There's something here. Think, Bog. Think.*

*If the supervillain won't play along, what about the monster? It's technically ours. It's Dominion property. A weapon, built for soldiers.*

He cleared his throat and raised his good hand, as though he were about to address a platoon.

"Hello? Hello! This is Bog, soldier of the Val Dominion! Private Monster, listen to my orders!"

The wall answered with a groan.

The pod was beginning to buckle. The tentacles outside tightened their grip, and the hull creaked and popped like an old ship in a storm.

"Whoa, hey now!"

A seam split along the ceiling. Cold air hissed in, bitter and stinking of ozone. And a single steel tentacle thrust through the crumpled plating, feeling blindly around the cabin like a fisherman's hook.

"Don't attack me!" Bog scrambled backward on his heels. "I'm your master! Holy Val, you're just as disobedient as Nia!"

The tentacle swung through the air, found the arm of the seat, and tore it out by the roots. Then it swept down and brushed against his leg.

"Gah! Get back! Seriously, stay away! Attacking Val is the superhero's job! You're on *our* side! When did you switch sides, you idiot?"

In a state of panic, he cycled through his usual, makeshift ideas: "Same as Nia," "superhero," "switch sides."

At that time, his hand drifted down to his belt and bumped something cold.

The collar.

He looked at it. It hung there, gray and oily, the spare he'd picked up from the rubble for no good reason.

*A collar suppresses a superpowered creature. Cuts off whatever drives it. Every collar in the Dominion is equipped with a control system that nobody can explain, and that obeys the Val.* His mind raced. *A monster that's been dormant for centuries, in a depot full of collars and calibration kits and prototype suppressors…*

It was a crazy, insane idea.

He was out of options.

"Oh, to hell with it."

Bog lunged. The tentacle was whipping past his shoulder, and he caught it behind the thick cable joint with his good hand and slapped the collar around it with a snap. The lock closed. A pale blue light pulsed along the band.

"Hahaha!" He was laughing, and he didn't know why. His face was running with sweat. "Suits you perfectly, you moron! Hahahahaha!"

*What on earth am I doing?*

The tentacle froze.

All of it froze, the whole writhing mass outside, each tentacle going rigid at the same instant. The pod stopped creaking. In the sudden silence he could hear only the thin shriek of air against metal and the pounding of his own pulse.

Then a flat, mechanical voice rang through the pod, from the collar, or from the walls, or from somewhere deep inside the thing that had swallowed him.

"General control system engaged. Awaiting orders, Commander."

Bog's laughter died in his throat.

"…Wait," he said slowly. "Who just spoke?"
