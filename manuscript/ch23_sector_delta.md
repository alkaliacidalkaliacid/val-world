## Chapter 23: Sector Delta


There were, in fact, several barriers of energy and matter in their path. Yet the discs Sonya opened acted as a master key, and they passed through every one of them with ease. To her, the distinction between a wall and a corridor seemed not to exist; she simply kept walking, unerringly, toward Sector Delta. Bog had no grasp of what her ability truly was. Nia, who had seen her share of clashes between superpowered individuals, was beginning to piece it together. It was an extraordinary power. If only this woman had fought alongside the heroes back in the day… Damn it. Then again, Nia reflected, she wasn't exactly the type.

They reached Sector Delta without a hitch. It was a treasure trove, or possibly a junk heap. Military supplies, or rather military relics, of the Val civilization lay scattered about, coated in dust and draped in cobwebs. Strictly speaking, the "cobwebs" were the filaments of a predatory, silicon-based slime mold, descended from a species native to some star system Bog couldn't quite place. The place looked less like a military depot than the storage room of an underfunded museum.

Nia frowned. It was in dire need of a cleanup.

Showing no sign of fatigue, Sonya turned to Bog. "Explain. What is all this?"

Bog began pointing out whatever he recognized. Unpacked firearms and artillery. Assorted vehicles. Hand grenades and defensive gear, identified by their packaging. Strings of anal beads, bells, collars, branding irons, paddles. "Unknown," he said gravely, nodding at a crate. "Unknown," he said of the next one, with equal dignity. There was even a run-of-the-mill planetary-core-destruction bomb. ("Say that again?" said Nia.) Expired beverages. "Unknown." Machine oil that was somehow still usable despite being centuries old. Obsolete uniforms. And a Collar-calibration toolkit, Class 5. ("Class 5? Is that higher or lower than Class 3? Oh well, this should do.") Among other things.

Sonya took an interest in a few items before finally pointing to a cocoon-like object in the corner, fashioned from a tangled mass of steel cables.

"Ugh…" Bog recoiled.

When Sonya asked for an explanation, he muttered, "If it's what it looks like, that's the space tentacle monster. A legend among soldiers. An autonomous weapon, developed ages ago by military scientists and modeled after an actual space-faring beast. Probably the most vicious thing in this whole warehouse."
