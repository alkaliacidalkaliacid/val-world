## Chapter 59: An Invitation


Bog weighed his options. He could wait in Florence for Nia to turn up, or he could have ventured as far as the old Alabama-Mississippi state line, taking a chance on whether he might catch the sound of her bells. In the end he decided to stay put for now, find a meal, a few drinks, and a bed, and let the rest wait until morning.

He had just pushed himself up from the bench when Priya spoke. If he didn't mind waiting until she finished her rounds, she said, he was welcome to a meal and a place to sleep at her home.

"Sure," said Bog, without a moment's thought.

Her house was one of Florence's dilapidated houses, patched and renovated into something livable. She shared it with her husband, their two teenage daughters, and her husband's mother. The husband greeted Bog warmly and thanked him for the medical supplies, and the daughters watched from the doorway with narrowed, wary eyes. The mother-in-law made her hostility perfectly clear, with a glare that could have stripped paint. Still, no one asked him to leave.
