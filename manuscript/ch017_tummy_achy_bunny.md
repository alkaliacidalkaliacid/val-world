## Chapter 17: Tummy-Achy Bunny


Some time later, Bog finally set the promised plan in motion, and the timing was far from ideal for Nia. However, with a wink, he said, "Don't worry. Val has a tool that can solve all your problems at once," and he held up a string of fist-sized beads with a lecherous glint in his eye. Nia gritted her teeth and glared at him. After about fifteen minutes of her sweet moans and curses, Bog stood back to admire his work with his head tilted and his arms crossed, like a tailor at the end of a long fitting.

"Huh. Everything fit. I figured I'd be improvising with a sewing kit."

Nia said nothing at all. She stood in the middle of the ruined rug on stilettos she had no business balancing in, the fishnet pulled taut over her thighs and the bustier cinched around a ribcage built to take tank fire. The bunny half-mask covered her eyes and the bridge of her nose and stopped just short of the scar. At her throat, a white pointed collar and a black bow tie sat prim and neat - and hid the null-collar completely. Behind her, the pom-pom tail hung from the end of the Val bead string, bobbing faintly whenever she breathed. The beads sat inside her, heavy and humming, working quietly at their lore-sanctioned task. Her bells were bare to the air and shivering, each one ringing a clear, bright, shameless note.

The silence went on long enough that the household robot rolled quietly out of the room.

"Tummy-Achy Bunny," Bog said, savoring it. "It has a ring to it. I think it's catchy. Practical, too. No one's going to mistake you for the Black Huntress in that. Not a chance. Those villains still hold a grudge against every hero who ever beat them, even with the world upside down. Walk into that meeting as yourself and you'd get a knife in your back before the handshake."

"*You♥*..." Her voice shook, and her fists clenched at her sides. The tail wobbled. "You are... the single... most...*♥*" The word she wanted had apparently not been invented in any language she knew, and the bells chimed through the gap.

"Good, that's the spirit. Stay in character." Bog crouched in front of her and tweaked the mask's long ears so they stood up straight, his thick green fingers surprisingly deft. "Backstory time. You're my pet, I picked you up cheap, you're a little dim and very bitter. You don't talk unless I tell you. If anyone asks, you're just a rental I'm showing off."

"I'm *not* a..."

"If anyone asks, squeeze candy."

She glared at him through the eyeholes with enough hatred to scorch the paint off a Val carrier. Under the mask, her cheeks were burning. She was aware of the weight of the beads, of the sway of the tail, of the wet shimmer of the dozens of small bells. She was aware that *Amara, Chidi, Daniel* had come to her mind slower than usual, and that the roll call had stuttered on the third name.

*It's a disguise. Reconnaissance. You will be walking into a room full of the people who can get you to the fortress.*

*This is leverage. This is a mission.*

"...Fine," she bit out. "But if anybody laughs, I'm breaking something."

"Nothing a good Val can't afford." Bog stood and offered his arm, grinning from ear to ear. "Ready, Tummy-Achy?"

She took it. Not out of choice, she told herself. The stilettos were simply that unforgiving.

Together they stepped out into the dust and orange light of the Wasteland, the soft chime of her bells carrying behind them on the wind.
