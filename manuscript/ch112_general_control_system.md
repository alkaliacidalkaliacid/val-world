## Chapter 112: General Control System

The monster's voice came out of the walls themselves, level and courteous, the way it said everything.

"Commander Bog. This unit requires a destination."

Bog was sprawled in one of the grown recesses with his boots off and his arms folded behind his head, a man in a bath he had no plans to leave. "Florence," he said.

"Logged. Destination: Florence."

"And when we get there, first thing — Priya goes home to her family. Before anything else. That's the priority."

"Logged. Priority: the return of Dr. Priya Raghavan."

At the word *Florence*, Rin lit up. She turned to Nia with her whole face doing it all at once — *That's wonderful* — plain as print.

Nia didn't answer it. Her face had gone serious, though her voice, when it came, was carefully casual, and her hands stayed folded in her lap, where she had put them and meant to keep them. "Bog. Have you met Dr. Hermes yet?"

It was Priya who answered.

"Dr. Hermes is d-dead," she said quietly. "I'm sorry. I don't know the whole of it — only that he's gone." She looked from Nia to Rin and back, and something in her expression said she had counted the cost of their journey anyway, every mile of it. "I know you came a very long way to reach him."

The compartment was very quiet after that. The membrane walls breathed, slow and even, and that was all.

Nia looked at her folded hands. The whole road — the mountain, the deal struck in the dark, all of it — had been aimed at a dead man. She had nothing to say that fit in this room, so she said nothing.

"Well," said Bog, and he sounded almost pleased about it. "Then we start the collar research from scratch."

Nia glared at him. From scratch — announced out loud, in front of everyone, like a shopping list. Their secrets, handed over with the boots off and the arms behind the head. Did he really trust this woman that much? Nia had known Priya Raghavan for the length of one ledge and one strange conversation, and Bog was giving her the whole of it, unasked. Just who *was* she, anyway?

Above them, the membrane wall dimpled, and a tentacle came down.

Fitted around it, near the tip, was a collar.

"Commander Bog," said the monster, out of the walls, out of everywhere. "Are you referring to this 'General Control System'? If you have a toolkit of Class 2 or higher, I can show you how to use it."

Every head in the compartment turned.
