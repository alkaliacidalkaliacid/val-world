## Chapter 38: The Collar Clicks


*Подожди-ка,* she thought, from somewhere far down in the dark. *Wait. As soon as my strength returns, my power…*

She was still holding on to that thought, the way a drowning woman holds a splinter, when she felt something cold slide around her throat.

It had come from nowhere. One of the tentacles had been busy somewhere behind her, and when it withdrew, it left a dull gray band wrapped around her neck, still slick with the monster's insides. The creature had swallowed every crate in that warehouse, she remembered distantly. Rifles, grenades, lockers, machine oil. Collars, by the dozen.

*Click.*

It was a small, neat, final sound. A lock closing.

The ember beneath her sternum, the one she had been guarding through every wave, went out. There was no pain and no warning. It simply stopped, like a lamp unplugged from the wall, and the place where it had been became a quiet, smooth emptiness that she could not reach and could not mourn.

Her mind, rinsed clean of everything but this one fact, understood it at once.

*So that is how it ends.*

Through the walls of the monster came a tinny, cheerful voice. "Oh, it fits you perfectly," said Bog. "I told you it would suit."

She wanted to tell him what she would do to him. In Russian, in English, in every language she had ever sworn in. She opened her mouth, and a tentacle traced the underside of her chin, and what came out was another breathless, ragged laugh.

The collar hummed against her throat. Everything the tentacles had been doing to her, patient and thorough, picked up again, and her body answered before she could think to stop it.

Once more, she reached the peak. Her back bowed, her toes curled against the cables, and a long, shaking breath left her that was almost a sob.

*Не сдамся,* she told herself. *I won't surrender.*

It was the last thing she still believed, and she did not know any more whether it was true.
