## Chapter 113: I Wasn't Asked

Bog sat up. The bath was over.

"You *knew* how to use the toolkit?" he demanded. "All this time? Why didn't you say so?"

"I wasn't asked," said the monster.

Somewhere behind Bog, Rin made a small, delighted noise, the sound of a girl watching a machine be exactly itself.

Nia was on her feet before the words had finished arriving. Hope went through her like weather — the collar, the whole long road, a dead man at the end of it, and now this, out of the walls of the thing that carried them. "Show me," she said. "Teach me how to use it. Now."

"This unit provides instruction at the Commander's order," said the monster.

Every eye went to Bog.

Bog looked at Nia. He looked at the collar-fitted tentacle, still hanging patiently from the wall. He scratched his head. "Well," he said, reluctantly, the way a man agrees to lend out something he had been enjoying owning. "All right. Show her." Then, lingering: "Only — what if it explodes? When we take them off. Maybe we shouldn't—"

"If done correctly, it will not explode," said the monster, flat as a ledger closing.

"But there might be some other risk," Bog said.

"You would lose access to basic functions," the monster said, "such as communication, sensory synchronization, remote control, loyalty monitoring, power conservation, storage, amplification, bursts, and resonance. But that is all."

Bog stared at the wall. He moved his lips, counting something off on his fingers — communication, sensory *something*, remote control — lost the thread around *resonance*, and gave up. "Those are *basic* functions?"

Nia said nothing. Her face had gone very still somewhere around *loyalty monitoring*, but she said nothing.

"Wait!" Rin burst out. "Hold on. Wasn't this supposed to just strip away a hero's power?" She pointed at the tentacle, at the collar on it, thinking out loud the way she did everything. "From what you just said — it sounds like it could actually *amplify* a hero's power. Or trigger a burst."

The walls were silent.

"Answer whatever questions they have," Bog said.

"Very well, 'presumed consort,'" said the monster. "I shall answer."

And it confirmed her deduction.
