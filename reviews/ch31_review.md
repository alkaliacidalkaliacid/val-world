## A. Canon / setting contradictions

- A1 (the stratosphere leftover; fix proposed as P1): Bog's interior - "*Right now we're still high in the stratosphere. The pull isn't too bad yet.*" This is a remnant of the user's original conception of the depot as a stratosphere structure (the root cause identified at the ch30 review, and the same leftover family as the ch22 rewrite). It now contradicts the mechanism established at the end of ch30: the monster was kicked out of geostationary orbit by the bomb's blast and is falling from orbit - the previous paragraph itself says the re-entry glow is only just beginning ("the darkness outside had begun to take on a faint, sickly orange glow"), i.e. they are at the top of the descent, not "high in the stratosphere", and orbital "pull" does not fade with altitude in the way the line implies. It is Bog's interior voice, and Bog is a layman - but unlike his other loose talk, this line states the physical situation the reader is relying on. P1 offers a minimal rephrase that keeps his reasoning beat (he still has time to think) without the stratosphere.
