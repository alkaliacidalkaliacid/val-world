## B. Internal consistency and voice

- Bog's priorities are perfectly in character and perfectly terrible: he inspects the fossil skull and wonders whether a creature that size "would have been tender" — the food errand (ch084) still running in his head while the apocalypse assembles itself behind him. His "Uh-oh" arrives exactly one beat after the reader's.
- "Out of breath for the first time in a week" quietly prices the cavern's scale and his alarm; this is the first thing in the whole descent that has made him run.
- The possession takes Rin's catchphrase with her: "*It's clobberin' time!*" in a voice "doubled, and tripled", with her eyes blazing violet behind the cockpit glass. The line the book has used for her finest hour (ch081) is now the horror beat, which is the correct economy — nothing new had to be invented to make it frightening.

## C. Craft

- The fossil library is the best new image in the descent: ribs "like the timbers of a half-built ship", a jaw "like a drawbridge", epochs of dead things in the stone — the unburied-dead theme given geological depth, so the purple's choice of materials feels inevitable rather than random.
- Bog's small voice — "Oh. This is the absolute worst." — is his ch081 soup-cold register ("This is bad") escalated one notch, and it lands harder than any shout because the chapter has just shown him kicking a three-skulled monster over like a stray dog.
- Ends on the charge, mid-roar: the cleanest cliffhanger of the split arc so far.
