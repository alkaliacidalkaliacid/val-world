## B. Internal consistency and voice

- B1 (minor observation, keep recommended) — the kick is narrated once ("Nia kicked him in the shin") but the dialogue assigns it two debts in sequence: "That is for the pod." … "That," said Nia, "is for what you just said." A second kick during Bog's toolkit defence has to be inferred. The comic rhythm carries it; flagged only so the ellipsis is known to be one.
- The doubled refrain is structural, not accidental: "a married woman's rump, a married woman's rump" for Priya, then "A married woman's backside. A married woman's, with a husband in an unknown city…" for Nia — the parallel is the accusation, and the kneading symmetry (left shoulder, right shoulder) is Bog's entire character in one stage picture.
- Rin picks up Nia's verdict from the previous chapter instantly — "Lucky Val! Get back!" — and her "It's clobberin' time!" is Darkwing's catchphrase inheritance (ch74: Nia gave an order in the same phrase). Both are in character for the girl who learns by echoing the adults she admires.
- Nia's flustered heart slips out mid-threat and she bites the sentence off — "...♥ ..." — the established ♥ mechanics under stress, used sparingly.

## C. Craft

- Bog studies the end of the world "with the polite attention of a customer examining a bill" and opens with soup-cold understatement ("This is bad") — his register against the scale is the chapter's comic spine, and it makes Rin's panic and Nia's fury both legible.
- The retreat choreography is pure Bog: both women over his shoulders, kneading both, trotting — outrageous, and tactically exactly what he said they should do (get back). Nia's "distant, clinical outrage of a woman filing an official complaint" is the correct narrative distance for it.
- Rin's finest hour is built properly: the bracing pose "like a child bracing for the end of a long, long game of tag", the traffic-cone orange (her colour — the Orange Clobberess), a thunderclap across three states — and then the bathos landing, "...Nia? I think I'm out of fuel," in a very small voice from a kneeling, dark suit.
