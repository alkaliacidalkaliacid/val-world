## B. Internal consistency and voice

- B1 (observation, keep recommended) — Bog's cosmology compresses ch067. The Mediatoress's own account (ch067): siddhis and astras thinned the fabric; the buried singularity was torn open by the wars; and "the fighting in a low-hanging moon over a land where the sun rises high… was the final blow" — the orbital depot battle generally. Bog's version pins the final break on one participant: "the Murderess went and opened a whole bunch of portals for the tentacle monster, and *that* broke it for good." Her portals were the depot fight's most visible power use, and Bog was there; the compression is in character (and quietly omits his own presence). The authoritative account stands untouched in ch067, and the part Nia's guilt turns on — the heroes' share — is identical in both. No fix proposed.
- B2 (observation, keep recommended) — Rin's "Why is the Pale Murderess *handled?*" invites a careless reader to conflate the woman Bog is literally handling with the assassin he calls "handled". The conflation is Rin's joke, not the text's error: the sleeper's identity is unambiguous on inspection (see A). Flagged so the near-miss is a known quantity.
- Bog discarding long answers "like a man going through his pockets for a lost key" and then, asked about Sofiya, wearing "the passing look of a man mentally patting his pockets for a thing he had been told not to mention" — the motif repeats deliberately and pays off.
- The hand beat closes the arc opened by ch079's jealousy inventory: the hand that stroked Priya's thigh absently is lifted, studied "as if it had been put there by a third party", and ceremonially moved to her shoulder. "Better?" "A little."
