## B. Internal consistency and voice

- B1 (minor flag, optional fix) - **The leash is never introduced.** Ch49 established cuffs only ("her hands cuffed in front of her"); the leash first appears here, already in Gorrak's fist and with a definite article: "in his enormous hand was the loose end of her leash." Ch51 then leans on it three times (in his fist; the green hand still curled around its end; Darkwing's gaze passing over "the leash"). A reader can infer camp handling (a leash clipped to her collar, lying loose while she was cuffed on the cot), but the text never says so. Options: (a) leave it - the inference is easy and the chapter is closed-chapter-adjacent (ch49 is already closed, so any fix should live in ch50); (b) ground it at first mention in ch50 with a half-line (e.g. the leash the camp had clipped to her collar trailing from the cot).
- B2 (record-keeping observation) - The Old Guards' armbands are established here for the first time: clean white armband stitched with a red cross and a number, lawful-combatant discipline ("the way the old Accord manuals taught it"), fighting in pairs. Already captured in `chapters/ch50.md` Facts; suggest also adding one line to the Old Guards entry in `lore/factions.md` when the arc closes, so the insignia is findable from the lore side.
