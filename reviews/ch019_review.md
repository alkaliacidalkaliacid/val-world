## B. Internal consistency and voice

- B1 (observation, no fix proposed) - Bog's excuse and the ch014 promise. Nia had begged "Don't tell anyone that you saw this"; Bog answered "My lips are sealed." Here, asked about the lateness, he announces to a villain, eleven raiders and the Pale Murderess: "Bathroom emergency. Hers, not mine. Sorry about that." The letter of the promise survives (he describes no scene, no towel, no jelly - only an emergency), and the announcement is operationally useful cover for why a pet-owner is late and distracted. But it spends her embarrassment publicly, in front of the one audience she would least choose, and she cannot protest without breaking character. Readings: Bog's pragmatism outranking his promise, or Bog knowing exactly how much of the truth is safe to sell. The tension is visible in the text either way; flagged so it is a known quantity, not an accident discovered later.
- B2 (observation, no fix proposed) - Nia whispers. The pet rules (ch017: "You don't talk unless I tell you"; ch018: "Don't speak unless I say so") are broken in the chapter's first exchange - she identifies Sonya to Bog in a breath of whisper, nails digging through his sleeve. It is a breach of the letter, in the only direction that matters: intelligence, delivered to her "owner" alone, that he needs to survive the meeting. Bog does not remark on it; his silence is his priorities. The scene plays as her competence overriding her costume, and the summary does not pretend otherwise. Left as written; noted because the rules were stated absolutely, and later chapters may want the whisper treated as an established exception (whispered intel to Bog is not "talking").
- Her litany returns under true threat - "Amara. Chidi. Daniel." in full order, after ch018's grocery-only surfacing. The instrument reads differently by context: groceries for humiliation, family for mortal danger. The progression tracked since ch012 remains coherent.
- Sonya's attention is the chapter's quiet engine: gaze fixed on Nia from the first paragraph, sharpening at the pat and the name, one step forward, then another as the chapter closes. The summary's Foreshadowing ("Sonya's attention is on Nia from the first minute") is exact.
