## A. Contradictions with canon / settings
No clear (hard) contradictions. Items:

- **A1 (possible tension, main point) - "buy a breeding pair".** Platoon Leader: "you've got three days to buy a breeding pair and do your duty to the Dominion." Read literally, a pair = two captives. But in ch03 Bog buys exactly one woman (Nia), and in ch04 his reserve application - submitted with her holo-photo alone - is provisionally accepted. The synopsis and characters/_minor_cast.md both record the ultimatum as obtaining "a breeding partner" (singular). The natural reconciliation is that the pair is Bog + partner, but the prose wording does not say that, and a first-time reader may expect two purchases. Wording fix proposed (fix 1).
- **A2 (low) - Tau Ceti locals "supposedly compatible with us".** Canon frames humanity as the Dominion's reproductive resource because humans reproduce naturally; it never says humans are the only compatible species, and this line is double hearsay ("The Elites say... supposedly") in a superior's joke. No contradiction, but it quietly makes humanity "the nearest compatible species" rather than a unique one. No change recommended; optionally record the Tau Ceti rumor in canon as unconfirmed hearsay.
- **A3 (low, logic) - "the military police will hunt you down".** Bog is on leave, in service, and reachable on his communicator - the Leader is literally talking to him. "Hunt you down" only makes sense as an assumption that Bog would run rather than report for the Tau Ceti posting. Reads as a superior's bluster; flagging the logic only.

- Timeline: the three-day deadline drives ch03's purchase and ch04's provisional acceptance; consistent with open thread T10 (the Leader has not closed the matter).

"Shouting at each other again" blurs that staging. One-phrase fix proposed (fix 2).

Of course it's a bog." - is his deadpan at its best, and ch04 pays the joke off again ("a planet that was all bog, which struck him as a terrible joke").
