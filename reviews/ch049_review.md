## A. Canon / setting contradictions

- **A1 (main point) - The soldiers call Nia "Pickle", an origin the current text no longer supports.** Line 24: '"Hey, Pickle," the soldier said, in thick, amused Val, slapping her thigh.' The line is original prose, but the ch001-048 pass has since fixed the word's ownership: "pickle" is Nia's one-way insult for Bog (origin ch004 - she calls him "you overgrown pickle" and Bog "filed it away"); Bog's pet name for her is "squeeze candy"; and every Bog-to-Nia "pickle" was scrubbed as a Claude hallucination pattern (ch014, ch016, ch039 rulings). As the text now stands, no one has ever called Nia "Pickle" and the squad has no source for the word. `chapters/ch049.md` Facts claim the soldiers' nickname is "(from Bog's word)" - unsupportable in the current text (Bog's word for her is "squeeze candy"). Needs a ruling: (a) change the soldier's form of address to a different nickname (e.g. "Bunny" - she is captured in the bunny costume), (b) keep "Pickle" and supply / record an origin, or (c) keep the line as-is and correct only the Facts note. Note: her litany adding *Pickle* later in this same chapter is a separate, consistent beat - that is her name for Bog, surfacing uninvited; she is not answering to it.

She had counted the second when the lieutenant's palm came down on her right buttock..." The antecedent of "the first one / the second" is unclear. Read as men, it would break the 413 arithmetic (see A-verification); read as palm prints, it chains cleanly into "the fourth palm print" and "the eleventh" later in the chapter. The lieutenant "ripped the tail out of her costume, examined it" and announced she was "already fitted"; by ch051 the tail is back in place ("The tail, which she had never forgiven, swayed in a lazy arc"). Its return is never shown. The implication is strong (he examined the fitting; the queue's amusement at the word "tail" in this chapter presupposes it), but a careful reader may trip on it.

The ten counted in ch049 are the men whose turns did come. Recorded so later chapters keep it straight.
