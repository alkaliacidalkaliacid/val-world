This instance is different in kind - Bog is insulting the monster, not naming Nia - and it is in the original source. But it does put Nia's signature word in Bog's mouth as a general-purpose insult. Options: (a) keep - Bog has lived with her vocabulary for months and picked the word up as slang; the borrowing is itself characterisation; (b) replace with a non-pickle insult to keep the word exclusively Nia's.
