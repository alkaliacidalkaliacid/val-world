Principle (user): relationships between Earth humans may stay "wife"; Val terminology is unified to "consort". When the narration or a Val speaker names the Val-side bond, use "consort"; "wife" / "husband" are reserved for Earth-human marriages. Recorded in the Glossary of `00_canon.md` (Consort entry).

Applied:
- ch012 (all 5 "wife" → "consort"; title "Seven Days" kept) — see `reviews/ch012_review.md`.
- ch002: "buy yourself a breeding partner" → "breeding consort" (supersedes the ch002 review's pair→partner ruling) — see the addendum in `reviews/ch002_review.md`.
- ch006 lines 116 / 118 / 134 / 136 and ch008 lines 40 / 48 ("wife" → "consort") — see the addenda in `reviews/ch006_review.md` and `reviews/ch008_review.md`.
- ch013 lines at 9:00 AM / 8:30 PM: Bog's "Morning, wife" -> "Morning, consort" and "Be my wife." -> "Be my consort." (ch013 review A1; the chapter had sat outside the earlier inventories' scope).
- Summaries synced where applicable (chapters/ch002.md, ch008.md, ch012.md).

Deliberately left unchanged under the same principle:
- ch005 line 26: "his wife" (Nia's human husband — Earth side).
- ch008 line 48: "I'm his partner." (shown to the user as a judgment call; not included in the ruling).

Records aligned as well (ch012 review D3 / D4, applied 2026-11-17): `03_open_threads.md` T01 and T13, and `01_synopsis.md` now use the consort wording. No pre-ruling wording remains in the records.

## 7. Units: yard–pound system as the general rule (ruled 2026-11-21)

Rule (user): adopt the yard–pound system for the main text and for the various configuration files, as a general rule. First applied the same day to the new consort body records (drafted in metric, converted before sync: `lore/consort_body_records.md`, the four cards, `02_current_state.yaml` — imperial only). Recorded in `04_style_guide.md` (Style, "Units") and in the head rules of `lore/consort_body_records.md`.

Scope of the rule: new prose and new records use feet/inches, yards, miles, pounds and inches (B/W/H); no metric units in new writing. Exceptions: archive originals (never rewritten); the tentacle monster's own non-Earth units (barium half-lives etc. — a deliberate device); quotations of earlier prose inside reviews/records keep the quoted wording; time, counts and percentages are unaffected.

Legacy stock audit (2026-11-21, regex count of meter(s)/metre(s)/kilometer(s)/kilo(s) in word form): **manuscript chapter files: 34 instances in 24 files** (plus the same text duplicated in part volumes and publication blocks); **record files: 30 instances in 23 files**, many of them quotations or discussions of the prose (reviews of ch050/051, the ch040 airspeed gloss in 05_timeline.md, the archive "110 kilos" note in the body records). Recurring items: Rin's suit is "nine meters / nine-meter" throughout (→ thirty feet / thirty-foot); the monster's repellent field is "30 meters" (ch120/122, → about a hundred feet / thirty yards — editorial choice); Sonya's sniper shot "four hundred meters" (ch034/035); the propulsion unit's "two hundred kilometers per hour" (ch040); "two hundred and forty kilos" of Val infantryman and "a hundred and ten kilos" of backside (ch050/051). **No retroactive sweep has been ordered yet**: conversion of the closed stock is a separate pass requiring per-instance editorial choices, pending the user's direction (options presented 2026-11-21: immediate pass / background pass alongside the AAVE pass / forward-only).
