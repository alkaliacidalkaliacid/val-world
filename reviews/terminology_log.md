# Terminology Log

Manuscript-wide terminology rulings and sweeps, kept so the chapter-by-chapter reviews (ch13 onward) do not re-flag lines that were changed — or deliberately left — under these rulings.

## 1. wife / partner → consort (2026-11-17)

Principle (user): relationships between Earth humans may stay "wife"; Val terminology is unified to "consort". When the narration or a Val speaker names the Val-side bond, use "consort"; "wife" / "husband" are reserved for Earth-human marriages. Recorded in the Glossary of `00_canon.md` (Consort entry).

Applied:
- ch12 (all 5 "wife" → "consort"; title "Seven Days" kept) — see `reviews/ch12_review.md`.
- ch02: "buy yourself a breeding partner" → "breeding consort" (supersedes the ch02 review's pair→partner ruling) — see the addendum in `reviews/ch02_review.md`.
- ch06 lines 116 / 118 / 134 / 136 and ch08 lines 40 / 48 ("wife" → "consort") — see the addenda in `reviews/ch06_review.md` and `reviews/ch08_review.md`.
- ch13 lines at 9:00 AM / 8:30 PM: Bog's "Morning, wife" -> "Morning, consort" and "Be my wife." -> "Be my consort." (ch13 review A1; the chapter had sat outside the earlier inventories' scope).
- Summaries synced where applicable (chapters/ch02.md, ch08.md, ch12.md).

Deliberately left unchanged under the same principle:
- ch05 line 26: "his wife" (Nia's human husband — Earth side).
- ch08 line 48: "I'm his partner." (shown to the user as a judgment call; not included in the ruling).
- ch10 lines 26 / 28: "housewife" / *Housewife.* (likewise not included).

Records aligned as well (ch12 review D3 / D4, applied 2026-11-17): `03_open_threads.md` T01 and T13, and `01_synopsis.md` now use the consort wording. No pre-ruling wording remains in the records.

## 3. "mate" → "consort" in Val speech + the rediscovery lore (2026-11-17)

Background lore decided at the same time and recorded in `00_canon.md` (World Rules): the Val had long depended entirely on the cradles (artificial wombs); natural reproduction was rediscovered only after contact with humanity, and the Elites hastily built the consort institution out of ancient texts — which is why it is somewhat off-kilter.

- ch05 line 22 (Bog): "Shame about your mate" → "Shame about your consort"

Records prose is likewise untouched (e.g. the "suitable mate" wording in Nia's card, which states the spirits' criterion, not Val speech).

## 4. "♥" typography normalization (ruled 2026-11-17, ch14 review D2)

Rule (user): every "♥" attaches directly to the preceding word - no space before a heart, anywhere in the book. The sources were inconsistent (part01 and the Burrow arc often wrote hearts as standalone spaced tokens; dump 2 attached them). Applied as a manuscript-wide sweep on 2026-11-17: every " ♥" became "♥" in all chapter files and all part volumes (archive untouched). Hearts before: 612; after: 612 (nothing lost). Spaces removed per chapter file: ch11 10, ch12 112, ch18 1, ch41 48, ch42 17, ch43 9, ch44 6, ch45 10, ch46 12, ch47 9, ch48 20, ch49 4, ch70 6, ch71 3 (part volumes received the identical transformation). Word counts of the affected chapters changed accordingly and their `chapters/chXX.md` headers and the session-to-chapter map were updated to the current file counts (wc -w). The rule is recorded in 04_style_guide.md.

The user identified Bog's reflexive "That's a lot of paperwork" as a Claude tic that had recurred until it annoyed — not a trait they set. It had also been canonised in the guides (04_style_guide.md "Desired texture" and bog.md's Voice line, both inherited from the Claude.ai-era summary). Applied 2026-11-19: the parenthetical removed from 04_style_guide.md and from characters/bog.md, and a Banned Patterns entry added to 04_style_guide.md barring "paperwork" as Bog's canned deadpan (the word remains fine in literal contexts, e.g. ch13's Dominion-forms scene, ch82's "It's in the paperwork").
