## B. Internal consistency and voice

- The chapter is Rin's POV and stays inside it rigorously: her inventory ("Head: attached. Arms: attached."), her recitation of the Boss's doctrine, her refusal to cry ("crying took too much of the air" — decided at nine, during the invasion) and the realisation that what she cannot bear is the *quiet*, because somebody has always come. The adoration of Nia ("grumpy, sharp-tongued Nia… grown to adore") continues her ch071–081 through-line.
- Bog's entrance is staged through her ears first — the voice echoing "like a stone dropped in a well" — then the bounding silhouette, then recognition: "It was Lucky Val!" Her name for him (ch081) doing the work of the whole relationship.
- Her "...Yes. Please. Yes." — wet, shaky, ashamed, against "a very good and well-reasoned rule somewhere about not being delighted to see him" — is the chapter's thesis in three words: the Val is the one who came.

## C. Craft

- The fall itself is slapstick with real stakes — ledge, spur, a very loud *bong*, the upturned-beetle flailing — and the purple's ambivalence (eating her / holding her) is kept unresolved in a single image.
- The long silence section is the bravest writing in the chapter: nothing happens, at length, on purpose, and it earns Bog's "Hey there!" as a genuine rescue rather than a gag.
- Ends on her surrender of dignity, not on a plot beat — correct for the POV chapter the arc needed between Bog's dive (ch082) and whatever is down here with them.
