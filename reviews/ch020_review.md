That conflicts with the concealment rig established in the ch017 review and relied on in the ch019 rewrite: for the ch017-021 outing the null-collar is hidden under a white pointed collar and a black bow tie, and ch019's staging was verified on exactly that basis (Sonya inventories the mask, ears, tail and bells; there is nothing at Nia's throat to see but the pointed collar and bow tie, and the camouflage holds). The ch020 wording predates the ch017 addition, so the tension is retroactive - but as the text now stands, the most dangerous observer in the book has plainly seen Dominion alloy at Nia's throat, which is a much stronger fact than the near-identification the chapter otherwise builds (posture, fighter's calluses, the scar). It also sits oddly with her ch019 line "Interesting accessory. Big for a rabbit." - the finger the Broker tells her to lower reads most naturally as still aimed at that "accessory" (the bells), not at a collar she was never shown to have spotted. Needs the user's ruling; proposal 1 below retargets the gesture to the bells and the visible rig, preserving the three-second menace and the eye movement, and leaving her suspicion to rest on posture / calluses / scar as the rest of the chapter does.
