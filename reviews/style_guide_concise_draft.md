# 04_style_guide.md — Style and Anti-Repetition (concise draft)

## Style
- Third person, mostly past tense, prose in English.
- Expand beat bullets into scenes of 500–900 words; end on a small hook.
- Texture: dark comedy, slapstick. Bog's obliviousness and Nia's dry
  fury drive the humour; quiet Bog humour against others' sincerity
  or horror.
- Units: imperial everywhere — feet/inches, yards/miles, pounds,
  inches, mph. No metric in new prose or records. Exceptions:
  archive originals untouched; the monster's own units stand; quoted
  old prose keeps its wording; time, counts and % unaffected;
  **Sonya alone measures in metric, in her speech and thought**
  (her 400-meter shot is never converted). History:
  `reviews/terminology_log.md` §7.
- Background: name specifics, not generics — a shagbark hickory
  with shaggy, weathered bark, not "a tree". Fit the detail to its
  place, person, time, season, weather and situation; keep it
  atmosphere, not exposition.
- Wording: vary nouns, descriptions and metaphor vehicles within
  a chapter; do not repeat the same word or image where a fitting
  paraphrase or metaphor exists. (General rule, user 2026-11-21;
  erotic application in `reviews/erotic_description_memo.md` §7.)

## Figurative language
- Nia's words "landing", bells humming at her speech: figurative —
  her vehemence, not literal effects. While collared she works no
  curse or power with physical force; her spiritual side works
  through the spirits' own rules.

## Voice
- Swearing: Val speakers (Bog at minimum) — "Holy Val". Earth
  humans keep "damn" etc.
- "♥": a cartoon symbol of Val domestication/conditioning in captive
  women's speech under arousal, fluster or conditioning; not unique
  to Nia or Sonya. Typing: attach directly, no space — "word♥".
- Nia: her "♥" is also fuelled by her growing genuine affection for
  Bog, which she cannot control.
- Sonya: short, laconic lines; Russian curses and thoughts in
  Cyrillic; "♥" even while cursing; metric in speech and thought.
  Narration name: **Sonya** (standard); **Sofiya** in formal/record
  contexts and where later prose already has it — sweep neither
  form. Never "Sophia" / "Sofia".
- The monster: flat, literal, formal. "Awaiting orders, Commander",
  "Please define '…'"; reports like clerical minutes — "Report",
  "Logged as…", "Vocabulary count: N". Courteous; never mocks.

## Recurring beats — use, but rotate
- The monster's vocabulary count; "Please define '…'"
- Sonya's denials logged as "cough" / "tactical analysis"; her
  "Не сдамся"
- Bog sneezing off-screen in the monster's scenes
- Old Guards: Rin's loud cartoonish energy vs Darkwing's dry laconic
  command; formal armbands; Rin's "Boss" impressions

## Time and seasons
- Story time is tracked in `05_timeline.md` (Day 1 = ch001, Bog buys
  Nia). **Never state an elapsed duration unless it derives from
  the timeline; if it does not derive, write none — not even
  vaguely.** An invented duration is a canon error.
- Seasonal cues broadly match the timeline's season bands, but the
  war has made the climate abnormal: off-season weather needs no
  justification. No month names or calendar dates.

## Banned / hard rules
- Never use "That's a lot of paperwork" as Bog's stock deadpan
  (a Claude tic, ruled out 2026-11-19). Literal paperwork is fine.
  Closed chapters stand.
- Add no new characters or plot threads unless the user specifies
  them.
- Sexual and degradation content: as bold, compelling and unique
  as the work's established tone (`00_canon.md`, theme).
