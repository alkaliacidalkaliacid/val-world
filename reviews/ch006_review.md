## C. Craft
- This is the book's first full interior chapter and its emotional thesis statement: praise, not pain, is the danger. "That's how they get you to the end of the road. Not with the pain. Pain I can survive." The forehead kiss - deliberately rhymed with Daniel's - and her breath catching against her will carry the whole Bog method (affection, not force, per her card background) in one scene.
- Structure is musical: sound-collage, then interior movements separated by ---, with two short third-person bridges (the kiss; the bath and bed) that land harder for being rationed. The refrains ("Hold the line", "Amara. Chidi.") work as rhythm, not repetition.
- The ending - her breathing slowing despite herself, one small silver chime, then nothing - is the strongest chapter ending so far, and it is achieved by surrender of tension rather than a hook. Correct choice for this chapter.
- Length 1,745 words is well above the guide's typical 500-900 scene range, but the chapter is a set-piece interior monologue, and chapters/ch006.md already records it as such ("Told in Nia's interior voice"). No trim proposed.
