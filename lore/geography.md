## Pack Layer (include in packs; ~450 words)

- **"Appalachia" in this work has two senses.** Broad: the ARC region — 423 counties across 13 states, from Southern New York to Northern Mississippi, including northern Alabama and northern Mississippi (Appalachian Regional Commission definition). Narrow: the mountain core. Story usage follows the broad sense unless a scene says otherwise. The Old Guards' "Appalachian branch" is the branch covering the broad region.
- **Florence, Alabama** — inside Appalachia (broad; northern Alabama). Attacked in the invasion but lightly (low strategic value), rebuilt by its scattered people; shelters refugees from Atlanta and Nashville. Priya's Free Clinics post and the Raghavan home are here. Main stage from ch055; the party arrived ch123 and is based at Priya's home (ch131).
- **The monster's hiding place** — woods at Florence's outskirts (ch123 on), Sonya aboard. Before that, a resting valley in northern Alabama beyond Val recon range (ch119–122).
- **Old Guards' bunker** — northern Mississippi: inside broad Appalachia, outside the narrow mountain core. Darkwing's base; Nia recovered there (ch053–054); evacuating by dawn at last report. It is **one of several Mississippi-side bases** (user ruling, 2026-11-21): losing it does not end the branch.
- **Bunker → Florence: 600 miles** (prose, ch077), inside the four days between the fall (ch030) and the reunion (ch076). The singularity mountain on this route is **Woodall Mountain**, Mississippi (user ruling, 2026-11-21).
- **The Burrow** — the monster's burrow near ruined Mobile, on the Gulf coast; Sonya's training site (ch041–048, ch070). Mobile → Appalachia ≈ 370–500 miles [est.]; Bog's propulsion flight at 150 mph ≈ 2.5–3.5 hours [est.].
- **The depot** — the Dominion's military depot in **geostationary orbit** (ch008 ruling; it is NOT above the Gulf of Mexico). Raided ch022–038; the fall from it begins ch030.
- **Bog's home** — a repurposed relay station in the Wasteland, on the edge of a dead freeway, in **South Texas** (user ruling, 2026-11-21 — the region is fixed at that level and no finer; air cars exist, so distance from the launch site is no constraint). The black market where he bought Nia is a ruined car park in the same South Texas theatre; the brokers behind it are headquartered across the border in Mexico (old setting), and the Dominion's ground-to-orbit ships departed from near **Boca Chica** on the Gulf coast.
- Season bands and the climate-anomaly ruling live in 05_timeline.md and 00_canon.md; this file does not duplicate them.

## Reference Layer

### Appalachia — the term
- Broad sense (default): the ARC region, Southern New York to Northern Mississippi, incl. northern Alabama and northern Mississippi. Florence and the Old Guards' bunker are both inside it.
- Narrow sense: the Appalachian mountain core. The bunker (northern Mississippi) falls outside it; Florence sits in the foothills at the region's western edge [est.].
- The factions file's "their own territories" for the Old Guards means cells across this broad region; the Appalachian branch is Darkwing's command.

### Florence, Alabama
- **What:** Small city in northern Alabama (Tennessee River); Free Clinics post; the Raghavan family's resettled home.
- **Where:** Northern Alabama, inside broad Appalachia; 600 miles from the Old Guards' bunker by the prose's figure (ch077).
- **Who & what:** Priya, Arjun, Dadi, Meera, Kavya; the clinic (ch055–068: the bear night, the Mediatoress's manifestation); Bog's Florence stay runs parallel inside the four-day frame, arriving ~fall+2 [est.]. The party returns openly in spacesuits ch123; Priya's home is the current base (ch131).
- **Hermes's laboratory:** a modest facility in Florence — a shed, not anything grand (solder, coffee, a Val casing, a blackboard sentence; user ruling, 2026-11-21) — where Hermes worked at two null collars for weeks; Bog reaches it through Arjun and takes the collars and the laser (ch069). Hermes's affiliation is the Free Clinics' Appalachian branch, which is no contradiction: Florence itself is inside broad Appalachia.
- **Status now:** Rebuilt and functioning; shelters Atlanta and Nashville refugees (canon ruling, 2026-11-19). Not a Val garrison town [est. — no garrison is mentioned].

### Woodall Mountain — the singularity mountain and caverns
- **What:** The mountain where the buried singularity's pillar stood (ch071–077); the Mediatoress buried the singularity into it (partial ch077, complete ch096). The cavern system below it is the stage of ch091–116 (Rin's fall, Priya's descent, the collar revelations).
- **Where:** Woodall Mountain, Tishomingo County, northeastern Mississippi, south of Iuka (user ruling, 2026-11-21) — the highest natural point in Mississippi at 806 feet, in the Appalachian foothills near the Alabama line; roughly 45 miles west of Florence [est., real geography]. On the bunker→Florence route.
- **Status now:** The singularity is buried (ch096); the backlash entity's activity there has ceased [est.].

### The monster's resting valley and Florence woods
- **What:** Surface resting place where the monster lay beyond Val recon search radius (ch119–122: showers, sunbathing, Sonya's inspection); afterwards the monster moved to woods at Florence's outskirts and hides there (ch123 on), with Sonya aboard and Rin's dead suit stowed.
- **Where:** Northern Alabama [est. — the ch119–122 scenes are northern Alabama landscape]; the valley-to-Florence move takes the rest of one day (ch123).

### Old Guards' bunker (northern Mississippi)
- **What:** Darkwing's fortified base of the Appalachian branch; Nia was brought there after the Mississippi camp and recovered under Darkwing and Rin (ch053–054); the base was evacuating by dawn (last known state).
- **Where:** Northern Mississippi — broad Appalachia, outside the narrow mountain core (user ruling, 2026-11-21).
- **Who & what:** Darkwing; Rin (based there before escorting Nia); Sgt. Gorrak of the Val Appalachia-6 security company was captured by the Old Guards in the camp fighting (ch053).
- **Status now:** Evacuation ordered at last report; the branch holds other Mississippi-side bases and survives its loss (user ruling, 2026-11-21).

### The Mississippi camp
- **What:** Where Nia was held inside the four days after the fall: caught, bought again, passed hand to hand, kept in a tent (ch049–053, ch078's enumeration). The Val Appalachia-6 security company held her leash (Sgt. Gorrak).
- **Where:** Mississippi; relation to the bunker [NOT ESTABLISHED] beyond the same state.
- **Status now:** Nia was extracted by Rin/Darkwing's people (ch053); the camp's fate [NOT ESTABLISHED].

### The Burrow (near Mobile)
- **What:** The tentacle monster's burrow, dug after its landfall on the Gulf coast (ch039); Sonya's taming/training site through the Burrow arc (ch041–048) and ch070; the monster also made the Nia duplicate there (ch039).
- **Where:** Near ruined Mobile, Alabama, Gulf of Mexico coast.
- **Status now:** The monster departed with the party inside it (ch111); the burrow's present state [NOT ESTABLISHED].

### Mobile and the Gulf coast
- **What:** Landfall point: the monster walked the seafloor and burrowed near ruined Mobile (ch039). Bog departed north from there by propulsion unit (ch040).
- **Distances:** Mobile → Appalachia ≈ 370–500 miles [est., real geography, per 05_timeline]; flight at 150 mph ≈ 2.5–3.5 hours [est.].

### Atlanta and Decatur, Georgia
- **What:** Atlanta was hit thoroughly in the invasion (contrast Florence, canon ruling 2026-11-19). Decatur (DeKalb County, Atlanta metro) is Priya's hometown: the family house in Decatur/Druid Hills, Emory (her hero-medicine studies; Arjun's Georgia Tech connection), Your DeKalb Farmers Market. Priya's research materials were lost in Atlanta.
- **Status now:** Atlanta destroyed; the Raghavans fled and resettled in Florence; Priya's parents survive elsewhere in the Free Clinics network.

### Nashville, Tennessee
- Mentioned only as a source of refugees sheltered in Florence (canon). Nothing else established.

### Baltimore, Maryland
- **What:** Nia's hometown. Cold Spring Courts (the clan's apartment complex; her awakening at fifteen in a fire there); Baltimore Polytechnic; Community College of Baltimore County; Kennedy Krieger Institute (her rehab work); University of Maryland ER (where she met Daniel). In the invasion she held the Baltimore evacuation corridor for nine days before defeat and capture.
- **Status now:** Post-capture, nothing current is established.

### Vesper
- **What:** A ruined megalopolis, Darkwing's former protectorate. Thorne Manor on the hill above the city, the Cave beneath it; the Chinese consulate where Rin's parents served; the city fell in the war's first month, taking the Manor, the Cave, Manfred, and the trainees who stayed.
- **Where:** A fictional city. Authorial background (user ruling, 2026-11-21): its geography roughly overlaps New York's — but this is **never stated in the work itself**; treat it as a silent model for layout and scale, not as a fact any text may name.

### Chengdu, China
- **What:** Rin's birthplace; her first ten years at her grandparents' house (grandfather a street repairman). Her parents left for consulate work; she joined them in Vesper at ten. Grandparents' fate unknown (communications dead).

### Novosibirsk, Russia
- **What:** Sonya's birthplace; the federal portal experiment that swallowed her as a baby; the government research-medical institute for superpower studies she attended growing up; later her FSB Alpha service and defection (locations of service [NOT ESTABLISHED] beyond the former Soviet sphere of her assassin years).

### Bog's relay station (the Wasteland)
- **What:** A squat, repurposed relay station on the edge of a dead freeway; Bog's dwelling before the purchase and the household of arc01–02 (ch004–021). Layout (basement water processor, stone shower cubicle, all-Earth kitchen, couch, helper-robot closet) is recorded in characters/bog.md.
- **Where:** "The Wasteland" of **South Texas** (user ruling, 2026-11-21; deliberately not pinned finer — air cars make exact distance from the coast immaterial). Bog takes Nia straight there from the market (ch004).

### The black market
- **What:** A black market in a ruined car park (ch001): diverted Dominion supplies, caged creatures, the women's row in metal lattice cubes. Bog, on leave, first sees Nia there (ch001) and returns to buy her (ch003).
- **Where:** A city in **South Texas** (user ruling, 2026-11-21; the city is not named). The brokers' organisation behind the market is headquartered on the Mexican side of the border (old setting, noted by the user 2026-11-21) — plausibly for access to the Boca Chica launch site.

### The depot (geostationary orbit)
- **What:** The Dominion's military depot — a fortress in geostationary orbit (ch008 ruling; the earlier "above the Gulf of Mexico" phrasing was an authorial error, corrected). Held the collar-removal tools and Dr. Hermes's research connection; Bog's unit connection and the raid of ch022–038; Sonya's betrayal and the fall (ch030) start the separation.
- **Where:** Geostationary orbit. Not a surface location; travel is by transport/portal (ch022).

### Boca Chica, Texas — the launch site
- **What:** The former SpaceX launch site at the southern tip of Texas, on the Gulf coast at the mouth of the Rio Grande. The country around it is heavily destroyed, but usable facilities remain (user-supplied frame, 2026-11-21). The ships that rose to the geostationary depot were not ordinary rockets, but they departed from near Boca Chica (assumed in the 2026-11-21 discussion).
- **Where:** Cameron County, South Texas; ~23 miles east of Brownsville on the only road in (TX-4); ~2 miles north of the Mexican border, with Matamoros across the river from Brownsville.
- **Note:** Recorded as the frame for the South Texas ruling; how much of it the prose ever names is a separate, later decision.

### Tau Ceti
- **What:** The posting Bog was threatened with if he failed to obtain a breeding consort within three days (ch002). Nothing else established.

### The Val homeworld
- **What:** Unnamed. Consort waste used as fertilizer slowly terraforms Earth's ecosystems toward it (canon, consort physiology). Nothing else established.
