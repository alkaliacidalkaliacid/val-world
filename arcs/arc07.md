## Purpose of This Arc

- Conflict / obstacle: The singularity's backlash; the party split across the fissure; everything Bog is hiding from Nia.

## Synopsis (150-200 words)
Two journeys converge. Nia rides toward Appalachia in Rin's suit, believing Bog dead; Bog escorts Priya up the mountain to finish the burying. The woods are already wrong — possessed animals, walking trees with faces in their trunks, all herding toward a pillar of purple light — and Nia chooses the mountain over her road west. On the ridge the stone giants rise, and from below a voice she knows speaks: Bog is alive. He flattens a giant with the laser, throws the weapon away, and serves as the Mediatoress's perch while Priya drives the singularity down — cornered, not finished. The reunion runs on his cover story (a missed bus); his evasion about Sonya is noticed. Then the backlash: the purple pours out as a mass of faces, Rin spends her blast and her fuel, the fissure swallows the dead suit with her inside, and Bog dives after her, leaving Priya asleep under Nia's guard. Below, the purple possesses Rin; Bog collars her, completes the bond, and declares her his third consort — confiding the plan Nia must not hear. Priya wakes above, herself, to a nurse's dreadful patience: "I think it's time you and I had a talk."

## Facts Established (now canon)
- The backlash herds, gathers, and wants bodies: it possesses animals, trees, stone, fossils and people, and physical force only ever disperses it. The burying remains incomplete (T03).
- Bog's cover story to Nia - the jetpack overshot to Alabama; her four days of mourning were a missed bus - is a lie she half-sees through. His answer on Sonya ("Handled... in good hands") was noticed as an evasion (T13).
- Bond mechanics: a null-collar works as a "tenant" in the body; bond-calm by direct contact (a handler's-manual footnote) tips a collar's fight against possession, and the full claim settles the bond - the collar shines steady white, and ♥ enters the bound woman's speech.
- Rin, twenty, is third consort: she knows Bog's plan for Nia (wife properly, many children), holds naming rights to his first child, and her suit is lost in the cavern. Darkwing knows none of it (T15).
- Both of Bog's heavy assets are gone: the laser (thrown into the ravine, overheated) and one Hermes collar (on Rin); one collar remains on his belt (T08).
