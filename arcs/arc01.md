## Purpose of This Arc
- Protagonist's goal: Bog - stay on Earth: buy a breeding consort within three days, join the reserves, settle. Nia - survive captivity intact and get back to her family.
- Conflict / obstacle: The null-collar and the power imbalance; her defiance against his patience; a three-day ultimatum that turns into an open-ended bargain.
- What changes from the start of the arc to the end: Master and captive become parties to a secret deal; her count reaches 413 (Bog), and she has demanded he act on the bargain.

## Synopsis (150-200 words)
Bog, a Val soldier, is given three days to buy a breeding consort and join the reserves, or be shipped to Tau Ceti. At a black market he buys the Black Huntress, Nia Okonkwo — a superhero who destroyed a tank division, fitted with null-collar, bells and rings, defiant: she has served 412 Val without conceiving and promises him dismemberment once the collar is off. What follows is a domestic war. Nia endures the household rituals by counting, by her litany of herbs, by her family's roll call — while Bog's patience and praise frighten her more than cruelty did. When he offers to trace her family by Val surveillance, she begs him not to, and instead they strike the deal that drives the book: she will live as his devoted consort and run his household; in return he will remove her collar and let her search for her husband and children herself, the bargain secret from Earth and Val alike. Bog confides secrets — supplies siphoned to the Free Clinics, Dr. Hermes, and the collar-removal tools kept at a depot in geostationary orbit. Nia accepts the consort proposal on her conditions; after a week she demands he act.

## Facts Established (now canon)
- The deal (T01): devoted consort service in exchange for collar removal and her own family search; secret from Earth and Val. Bog has not looked up her family, at her request.
- Bog diverts Dominion medical supplies to the Free Clinics as a paid side channel; Dr. Hermes is his contact there.
- The collar-removal tools are kept at the Dominion's military depot in geostationary orbit.
- Nia's intake profile: powers awakened rescuing a child from a fire; a therapist's assistant who held the Baltimore corridor nine days; husband Daniel, children Amara and Chidi; served 412 Val without conceiving - her spirits open the door only for a worthy mate.
- Bog's body carries a long scar regeneration never touched, and a thin-plated spot under his left shoulder blade (T11).
- Consort physiology begins: her body rebuilds itself on Val protein, and the first ♥ escapes her.
