## Purpose of This Arc
- Protagonist's goal: Nia - survive the Val camp with the toolkit and her secrets intact; then reach Dr. Hermes and the collar's removal.
- Conflict / obstacle: The unit and Gorrak; and her own grief, misfiled as fact - she believes Bog died in the pod.
- What changes from the start of the arc to the end: The unit's prisoner becomes the Old Guards' guest, routed to Appalachia under Rin's escort; Darkwing enters the board.

## Synopsis (150-200 words)
Separated from Bog, Nia's pod lands her in worse hands: captured by a Val unit in Mississippi, she endures a night in their camp, counting — the unit's ten bring her to 423 — and holding one door shut in her mind: she will not say Hermes, Appalachia, or collar. The toolkit sits unrecognized on the supply stack. Rescue comes as slapstick war: a fuel drum erupts and Rin's nine-meter suit steps through the trees — "It's clobberin' time!" — with the Old Guards pouring after it, and Darkwing himself, the Knight of Vesper, alive at sixty, dropping soldiers with cables and sneeze powder. Gorrak, who never got his turn in the queue, is counted separately — 424, reconnaissance — and slammed senseless. Afterwards Nia meets Rin properly: twenty, Darkwing's sidekick, the only one who stayed when Vesper fell. Darkwing keeps her toolkit unlogged, asks almost nothing, and hears her edited report — a Val bought her, a villain turned, he died in the pod. He sets her road: Rin escorts her to Appalachia and Dr. Hermes - a freed Bound could turn the war. Nia imagines the collar off — and a fat idiot on a couch. "He's dead."
