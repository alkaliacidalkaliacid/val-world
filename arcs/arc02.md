## Synopsis (150-200 words)
The postponed rendezvous finally happens. Bog first keeps Nia home through a humiliating digestive crisis — the consort diet remaking her body — and photographs her beside the evidence, a keepsake of her as a person, for after the deal; cornered by his sincerity, she poses, smiles despite herself, and does not curse him once. For the meeting he dresses her as "Tummy-Achy Bunny", a silent pet rental whose costume hides the null-collar under a pointed collar and bow tie. At a ruined gas station they meet the Broker and his assassin: Sonya, the Pale Murderess, who is simply there between one breath and the next, and whose attention fixes on Nia's neck and what the bow tie covers. The Broker wants Bog's depot intelligence; Bog bluffs knowledge he does not have. The Broker's counter-offer — hand over the coordinates and Sonya retrieves the supplies unnoticed — would erase Bog's part in his own plan, so he talks himself and the bunny aboard as "quality control". His real design: set the villains and the Dominion at each other and lift the collar tools in the confusion. Sonya's condition for the rabbit: if she bites anyone, she takes her tail.
