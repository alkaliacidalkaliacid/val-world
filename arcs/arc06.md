## Purpose of This Arc
- Protagonist's goal: Bog - wait in Florence for Nia; then, conscripted, escort Priya west. The Red Mediatoress - rebury the singularity, in secret.
- Conflict / obstacle: The possession outbreak in Florence; the Mediatoress's contempt for the Val who heard everything; the secrecy the mission demands.

## Synopsis (150-200 words)
Bog reaches Florence first and learns what Nia still does not know: Dr. Hermes is dead, killed by a black bear a month ago. Waiting at the free clinic, he is taken in by Priya Raghavan, a doctor of superhuman medicine who has seen shapes in empty rooms since childhood. That night the rumor walks in: a bear with a purple aura and a human in its jaws — possessed, people say, by a bhuta, one of the war's unburied dead. The fight runs through the family home, bear to husband to grandmother, until Priya's terror detonates into light and burns the purple off everything; her sari burns into a crimson-and-black costume, and the Red Mediatoress speaks through her. The cosmology lands: the heroes' wars thinned the world's fabric and tore open a buried singularity, the depot fighting dealt the final blow, and the unburied dead are leaking through. The mission is to rebury it, in secret. Bog, who heard everything, is spared at Priya's plea and taken on as her subordinate. He arms himself from Hermes's shed: the laser, and two null collars. Interlude: Module Nine completes in the burrow; Sonya's count reaches 335, still second, nine behind Nia.
