## Purpose of This Arc
- Protagonist's goal: Bog - steal the collar toolkit and get Nia out alive. Nia - keep the depot's weapons from reaching the Broker's masters, whatever it costs the mission.
- Conflict / obstacle: Sonya's betrayal, the waking monster, and the depot coming down around all of them.
- What changes from the start of the arc to the end: The toolkit is secured but Bog and Nia are separated; Bog commands a Dominion superweapon; Sonya is captured and collared.

## Synopsis (150-200 words)
The raid goes wrong at once: Sonya's portal tunnel delivers them into the depot warehouse, but her probing blows Nia's cover in everything but name. Bog finds the Class 5 collar-calibration toolkit among the relics — and Sonya piles her loot on the cocoon of the legendary space tentacle monster. When Nia's conscience balks at arming the Broker's masters, Bog warns Sonya off, and she turns on them: she meant to dispose of them all along. Her portals sever and return everything, but the waking monster is coreless, regrows two tentacles for one, and grows by eating the depot itself. In the escape bay Sonya steals the last clean pod for herself; Bog retakes it, puts Nia and the toolkit aboard, and is swallowed in his own pod. Falling to Earth, he slaps his belted spare collar onto the monster — and the General Control System names him Commander. Ordered to neutralize Sonya, Bog refuses the kill and invents a different way: the monster tickles her into surrender and collars her. Bog sets her standing orders — sleep, food, safety, and taming, indefinitely — has the monster build a duplicate of Nia, and flies north to find the original.

## Facts Established (now canon)
- Sonya's portals come in entry/exit pairs and can snap shut mid-transit, severing what passes. The counts (four quiet, six all-out) are Nia's estimates, not a canon cap.
- The monster is a sealed Dominion weapon: coreless, regenerating, growing by absorbing matter, and blind-seeking the strongest presence. Collars carry a General Control System that obeys the Val who fits them; Bog is its Commander.
- Sonya survived inside the monster in an absorbed escape pod, after detonating a depot charge herself; captured, she is collared (powers out) and fitted with rings, bells and a bead string. Her Russian profanity log opens at 312.
- Bog would not order the kill: Sonya's "neutralization" becomes the taming regimen of ch40's standing orders instead.
- The monster built a duplicate of Nia from Bog's description (imperfect work). Bog's instruction: "Don't tell her about this part" (feeds T13).
