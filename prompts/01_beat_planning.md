# Prompt 01: Beat Sheet Planning (use before writing prose)

## What to include
- 01_synopsis.md
- Current arc summary
- 02_current_state.yaml
- 03_open_threads.md (open section only)
- Previous chapter summary

## Prompt
```
You are in charge of structure. Do not write any prose.
Using the materials below, create only the beat sheet for the next chapter.

Requirements:
- The POV character must have a clear goal and an obstacle blocking it
- There must be at least one irreversible change between the start and end of the chapter
- Change at least one of these from the previous chapter: location, opening style, type of conflict, type of ending hook
- Prioritize resolving open threads where possible; plant at most one new thread
- No safe, generic developments (resolving everything through a calm talk, or help arriving by coincidence) are allowed

Follow the fields in chapters/_template_beat_sheet.md exactly.
If the beat is weak, say why and suggest an alternative in 3 lines at the end.

[Paste materials here]
```
