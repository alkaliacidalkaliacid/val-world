## Prompt
```

The beat sheet's specifics list is the starting set; keep specifics as atmosphere, not exposition.
Where the same noun would recur within a passage, vary it with a fitting paraphrase or metaphor instead of repeating it. Deliberate motifs, liturgy, and canon terms are excepted.

Target length: [ ] words
Output the prose only. No commentary or summary.
```
