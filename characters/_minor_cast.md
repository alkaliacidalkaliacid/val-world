# Minor Cast

> One shared file for minor characters, from the world doc, the character docs, and the Bog & Nia summary (all archived). Promote anyone here to their own card when they become recurring.

## Dominion side
- **Platoon Leader** - Bog's superior; contacts him by arm communicator. Issued the three-day ultimatum (obtain a breeding partner or be posted to Tau Ceti).
- **Sgt. Gorrak** - Val sergeant of the Appalachia-6 security company. Lost a dice game, took Nia's leash, was knocked out by Rin's suit and captured by the Old Guards.
- **Household robot** - Bog's helper at the relay-station home; squeaky wheel; obeys Bog; tactful. He is one of three helper robots Bog owns: this one is in regular service, the other two stay in the closet unused (ruled 2026-11-17, ch007 review).

## Human side
- **Dr. Hermes** - Super-genius mutant doctor (no combat powers) at the Free Clinics' Appalachian branch; claimed he could unlock collars with the right tools. **Dead** - killed by a (bhuta-possessed) black bear a month before the Florence sessions. Priya's colleague; Arjun helped him analyze Val medical gear.
- **Arjun Raghavan** - Priya's husband; PhD in engineering (Georgia Tech, advanced medical devices). Married to Priya for 19 years (canon, 2026-11-18). He married her in disregard of the reputation her stutter and her strange sense had given her in the community, for her intelligence and her character (ruled 2026-11-19). Stayed in Florence with the family and clinic. Habit: tests everything himself first ("test first, verify afterward"). Lent Bog the overheating Val medical laser.
- **Dadi** - Priya's mother-in-law; openly hostile to Bog; threatened to find him if Priya is hurt. She carries much of the running of the household (Priya is busy at the Free Clinic); she taught Priya cooking from a blank slate — the bride and Arjun had both eaten carelessly as graduate students — and the teaching never became a conflict. (Household role ruled 2026-11-19.) She was at first against Arjun's match when she heard Priya stuttered, but Priya's character changed her mind, and she now truly treasures her daughter-in-law. Her hostility to Bog is protective on principle — an unrelated man in the house, familiar with Priya — sharpened by what she has heard of how the Val treat women (and her insight there is, in fact, 100% correct). She accepted his visit at all only out of mutual respect with Priya, bending her own rule because Priya had brought him home as the Free Clinic's benefactor. (Ruled 2026-11-19.)
- **Kavya and Meera Raghavan** - Priya and Arjun's teenage daughters (Kavya the younger, Meera the elder), in Florence. Meera is reserved and wary, her gestures speaking for her (the raita slid an inch toward Bog in ch060 was a treaty); she watches over her sister. Kavya is blunt and direct (fork held like a weapon, the point-blank "Do you have a mother?"), with a child's unguardedness still in her. Both were small children when the family fled Atlanta; Florence is effectively their hometown. (Adopted 2026-11-19.)
- **Daniel Okonkwo** - Nia's husband, former emergency-room physician. Missing since the fall of the eastern seaboard; Nia believes he may be alive. (See T01 / Nia's card.) Traits accumulated from Nia's memories in the prose (recorded 2026-11-17, ch009 review, at the user's direction): lean, soft around the middle - he apologized for it and she teased him for it, and he laughed (ch009). Gentle and funny; "got the proportions right" (ch007). His mouth tasted of coffee and mint (ch008). He kissed her forehead after long shifts, when she came home with blood under her nails (ch006); his thumb rubbed the scar on her cheek (ch006) and traced her back on nights she could not sleep (ch007). He worked double shifts (ch006, ch007) and still, every night, saw a pile of dishes and just started - he never waited to be asked, and would have fixed Bog's whole kitchen in an afternoon (ch007). His words over Chidi's crib: "Kind-hearted. Cherished." (ch006). He cooked jollof - tomato, Scotch bonnet, thyme (ch007, ch008). Per Nia, he never asked her for anything like the household ritual, and would have been horrified by it (ch007).
- **Bunker nurses (Old Guards)** - Amateurs: an ex-dental hygienist, a second-grade teacher, a retired postal worker with a rosary, a nineteen-year-old trainee.

## Antagonists and neutrals
- **The Broker** - Middle-aged man in a suit, long coat and glasses, with a smooth boardroom voice; leader of the supervillain-raider group that hired Sonya for the depot raid. Not seen since the rendezvous; possibly financed by the League of Shi (Darkwing's suspicion, unconfirmed).
- **Mister Ruin** - Former supervillain; demolition expert who weakens the structural integrity of materials; sells sabotage to the highest bidder.
- **Madame Morrow** - Former information broker; extracts fragments of memory through physical contact; keeps a private archive of secrets.
- **Gravemind** - Telepath who can influence crowds but not reliably control disciplined individuals; leads a small fearful following in a ruined industrial district.
- **The Jackal** - Non-powered master thief and escape artist; trades stolen alien equipment and changes identities.
- **Saint Voltage** - Disgraced energy manipulator who formerly claimed to be a hero; supplies power cells to Val officers in exchange for protection.
