## PACK LAYER

- Role: Protagonist. Low-ranking Val soldier, on leave on Earth, provisional reservist (no rank beyond that — "sergeant" was a Claude invention, struck). 7.5 feet tall.
- Desire: To settle on Earth; someday a fine Earth-style house of his own. Ending direction: he builds a harem and is happy, with Nia as his number one.
- Lack / wound: Grown in a cradle — no mother, and his species has no concept of self-sacrifice. A large old scar and a thin-plated spot under his left shoulder blade (origin unknown).
- Strength, and how it backfires: Cunning beneath a haphazard, easygoing surface; physically a Val, far stronger than humans. His improvisations keep succeeding beyond his rank — and keep escalating his exposure.
- Self-deception: He presents his devotion to Nia as mood and appetite, while acting on it consistently at real cost; he never frames his own risks as sacrifice.
- Voice: Crude, easygoing, lewd, haphazard; oblivious deadpan against other people's horror. Swears by "Holy Val". He never calls Nia "pickle" (that is her insult for him); his own endearment for her is "squeeze candy".
- Sample lines: "Business, actually." (ch56) / "Oops. I only said that to set the mood." (ch12) / "Just the tip. We will work up to it." (ch92)
- Habits and tells: Earth-culture enthusiast whose knowledge is confidently skewed; proud collector of mismatched Earth kitchen gear; fixes and rewires things himself. Plays dumb. Keeps secrets by cheerful evasion, not by silence. Tames through affection and feeding rather than force.
- Under stress: Stays loose and joking; improvises; escalates his commitment instead of retreating.
- Relationships: Nia — partner and future primary consort; a devotion he will not name. Rin — his third consort, collared by his hand; she knows his secret plan for Nia. Sonya — the captive he handed to the monster; to Nia she is "handled, in good hands". Tentacle monster — he is its Commander. Platoon Leader — his superior, reached by arm communicator.

### Provenance and rulings

### Home
- A squat, repurposed relay station in the Wasteland, on the edge of a dead freeway (established ch04 — he takes Nia straight there after the purchase; it is already his dwelling). Do not write him as homeless or quarterless. What he dreams about in ch01 ("a house. A real one, with walls") is a different, finer Earth-style house where he can truly relax. (House dream: user setting, 2026-11-17.)
- Layout (accumulated from the prose, recorded 2026-11-17, ch09 review): a basement with a Val water processor that could fill a lake (ch09, ch13); a stone shower cubicle at the back of the house, big enough for him and cramped for two (ch09); a kitchen of all-Earth, mismatched gear inventoried with pride in ch05 — toaster from Ohio, rice cooker from Osaka, dish rack next to the fridge, knives in the cupboard with the cereal, sink at chest height for Nia and stove near her chin, outlets wired to something that hums (ch07) — on a power system Bog rewired himself because "the wiring was ridiculous" (ch05); a living room with an Earth-made couch "comfortable beyond all reason" (ch05); a closet holding the two unused helper robots (ch07).
- Helper robots: he owns three in total. One — the squeaky-wheeled, tactful one that obeys Bog — is in regular service; the other two stay in the closet, unused. (Ruled 2026-11-17, ch07 review.)

### Earth attachment
- Since coming to Earth he has grown thoroughly attached to Earth culture, and to women. His knowledge of Earth culture is heavily skewed and strange — pieced together from markets, occupation life and fragments, and confidently off in ways he never notices. Played for comedy: he is an enthusiast, not an expert. (User setting, 2026-11-17.)

### Business and the deal
- He secretly diverts Val medical supplies to the Free Clinics' Appalachian branch. It is paid business, not charity: the Clinics pay well for Val gear (ch08: "They pay a fortune"), and in ch56 he corrects Priya's "volunteer" with "Business, actually." Whether Bog profits from the black market itself remains deliberately ambiguous (ch01 ruling).
- Under the standing deal, he has NOT looked up Nia's family, at her request.

### Left arm (injury chronology)
- At the orbital depot (ch26) his own stun shot was redirected through Sonya's portal and brushed his left shoulder, numbing the whole arm. It stayed numb through the fall (ch28–36), began to tingle at landfall (ch39), and he was still working the numbness out in ch40. Ruled 2026-11-18 (ch55 review): by Florence it is only a light, lingering numbness — not a disabling injury, and not something he favors or cradles. In ch56 Priya spots the faint awkwardness in the arm's movement and treats it (ch56 review).
