# Character Card: [Name]

> How to use: Create one file per character (e.g. characters/yuna.md)
> Include in context: Only the cards for characters appearing in that chapter. Never include all of them.
> Do not write current state (location, emotion, inventory) here. That belongs in 02_current_state.yaml.
>
> **Two-layer structure.** A card has a *pack layer* — Core, Voice, and any
> always-needed facts, target ≦ ~300 words — which is what goes into a chapter
> context pack, and a *reference layer* — Background / Personal History and other
> deep material — which is pulled only when a chapter touches it. Rich cards
> (1,000+ words) are fine as files; the pack layer is what must stay small.
>
> **Building a character's past (method, established 2026-11-19 with Nia and Sonya).**
> 1. *Harvest*: collect every fact already in the files (card, lore, manuscript,
>    archive sources), each with its source. Nothing is invented at this stage.
> 2. *Gap list*: list what the sources do NOT establish (origin, family, turning
>    points, tastes, relationships…). Present the gaps to the user.
> 3. *Rulings and proposals*: the user rules on what touches the story's core.
>    Muse's suggestions are written into the card marked [PROPOSAL] and lose the
>    mark only when the user adopts them. Never canonise an assumption.
> 4. *Axes*: organise the past on a few axes chosen for that character — the
>    contexts that decide who they are (Nia: family / hero work / her job.
>    Sonya: military service / the underworld career / the other-side experience /
>    her Russianness). Material that serves no axis is cut.
>    *Personal life is core, not filler (user, 2026-11-19)*: what forms the
>    person as a human — private life, tastes, habits, relationships outside
>    the plot — is what determines their actions, judgments and autonomy. Do
>    not fill only the parts that touch the main plot, or the character becomes
>    a function that merely performs a role. Never treat the personal-life
>    items as skippable leftovers.
> 5. *Compress*: prune rather than inflate. If a file grows too large, cut
>    information while preserving context — context is what decides the person.
>    Tastes and preferences are written as systems, habits and philosophy that
>    reveal character (why this brand, why this style), never as bare lists of
>    single items.
> 6. *File*: the finished past goes into "Background / Personal History" in the
>    reference layer, headed by a note naming its axes and its provenance.
> Consult the user in the profile-consultation chat before rewriting a card.
